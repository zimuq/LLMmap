---

## D — The question

> **Break the single `hard_subset` number (mean two-logit restricted
> accuracy over the 65 structural near-relative pairs, `METHOD.md §8`)
> into its 65 pair-level components, for `paper8`, `coverage`
> (`cvar_max`, γ=0.1), and `joint energy`, at matched `k`. Does the
> ≈0.4–0.5pp aggregate gain (D010, k=8) come from most pairs improving a
> little, a few pairs improving a lot while the rest are flat, or some
> pairs improving while others get worse (redistribution the aggregate
> would hide)?**

### What is and is not known going in

- The aggregate `hard_subset` numbers are already reported (D010, k=8:
  paper8 .9637, coverage .9644, joint energy .9685) and are small in
  absolute terms — the entire random-to-oracle range on this metric is
  only ~7.5 points (D008's own finding, the reason D008 was INCONCLUSIVE
  by the letter).
- Every run in D009/D010/D012/D013 already stores a `per_hard_pair` list
  of 65 floats per run (verified present, 2026-09-22). No new training,
  selection, or generation is needed — this is a read-and-reorganize job.
- The two previously-named difficult pairs (Falcon3-10B/7B,
  Phi-3-medium-128k/4k) are **not** derivation pairs — checked against
  `results/D001/model_metadata.csv`: both have an empty `base` field
  (no declared fine-tune parent/child relationship between the two
  models in the pair). They match on `lineage` only (`falcon-3`,
  `phi-3` respectively) — i.e. they are siblings released in the same
  model family/generation, not one derived from the other. Of the full
  65 structural pairs (37-model universe), 6 also share a literal `base`
  field (true fine-tune-of-shared-parent pairs), and those 6 are a
  subset of the 65 `same_lineage` pairs, not a disjoint category, in
  this dataset.
- What is *not* known: whether the aggregate gain is broad or narrow: no
  per-pair breakdown beyond the two named pairs has been reported.

---

## What counts as an answer

Report, per pair, per method, at `k`=8 (the project's standard headline
budget) and `k`=1 (smallest budget, for contrast):

- mean accuracy across the already-run 5 seeds, with a bootstrap CI
  computed the same way the project already computes per-cell CIs
  (paired at the config-bootstrap level, per D009's convention) —
  **note going in that these will be noisier than the aggregate**: each
  pair's number comes from ~50 evaluation traces (2 models × 25 test
  configs) × 5 seeds, an order of magnitude less data than the 65-pair
  average. State this caveat in `R`, don't let a narrow per-pair CI be
  read as a null result.
- Δ(joint energy − coverage) and Δ(coverage − paper8) per pair, flagged
  by sign — **explicitly list any pair where Δ is negative** (joint
  energy or coverage does worse than the comparator), not just the
  positive ones.
- Where the two previously-named pairs rank in this list — are they the
  hardest under every method (consistent with "structurally hard
  regardless of selection"), or does their rank change by method
  (consistent with selection-algorithm-dependent difficulty)?

**Not required:** a hardness ranking presented as an adopted definition
(`H_ref`) — see Constraints. **Not required:** worst-class per-pair
analysis, or any new `γ`/`k` combination beyond what D009/D010/D012/D013
already ran.

## Required steps

**S0 — Confirm consistent pair indexing across the four source files.**
Each of D009/D010/D012/D013's runs was produced against the same frozen
37-model universe (I6), so `near_relative_pairs(models)`'s 65-entry
index→(model_a, model_b) mapping should be identical across all of
them. Verify this explicitly (e.g. assert the same `models` list, or
the same derived pair-name list, in each file's metadata) before joining
any numbers across files — a silent reordering would silently corrupt
every downstream row. Report the check's outcome in `R` even if it
merely confirms the expected.

**S1 — Core deliverable: the 65×3 table at `k`=8.** For each of the 65
pairs, report `paper8`, `coverage` (γ=0.1), `joint energy` mean accuracy
(5-seed mean + CI) and the two deltas above. Source: D009's `paper8` and
`cvar_max` runs at `k`=8, D010's `joint_energy` runs at `k`=8. Sort for
readability by `paper8`'s accuracy (hardest-under-paper8 first) —
label this sort as a display convenience, not a hardness claim.

**S2 — Contrast table at `k`=1.** Same construction, `k`=1, to see
whether concentration/spread looks different at the smallest budget.

**S3 — Optional, only if cheap: fold in the existing γ grid.** D012
(JointGreedy, γ∈{1.0,0.25,0.1,0.05}) and D013 (GreedyCover,
γ∈{1.0,0.5,0.25,0.1,0.05}) already have `per_hard_pair` stored for every
γ×`k` cell they ran. If it adds no new computation beyond aggregation,
report per-pair Δ across γ for the two named pairs specifically (a
natural extension of the review's existing 2-pair table) — but **do not
build a denser pair×γ×`k` grid than what these two D's already ran**,
and label every cell with which source D it came from.

**S4 — Explicitly separate three things in `R`, per the review's
typology (do not conflate them):** (a) this D's 65-pair table is the
existing structural set `N` (metadata-defined, fixed since D001/D008
specifically to avoid scoring a selection algorithm against a hardness
definition drawn from its own tensor) — not a hardness-ranked
benchmark; (b) the two previously-named pairs, shown in context among
the other 63, not cherry-picked; (c) note explicitly that this D does
**not** construct a frozen empirical hard set (`H_ref`) — that requires
a reference protocol, an independent development split, and a
pre-registered threshold, none of which exist yet, and adopting one as
a primary endpoint is a separate human decision.

**S5 — Report descriptively; reuse existing statistics code.** No new
significance-testing framework — the same bootstrap/CI procedure already
used for aggregate metrics in D009–D014, applied per pair instead of
averaged over pairs. Do not invent a new test.

## Constraints

**Applicable invariants:** I6 (every number sourced from
D009/D010/D012/D013's already-frozen runs; no re-selection, no
re-training, no re-generation, no re-embedding).

**Out of scope:** constructing `H_ref` (S4c); any new `γ` or `k` value;
worst-class per-pair analysis; any claim that a pair is "solved" or
"intrinsically hard" from these numbers alone (per-pair CIs will be wide
— report them, do not round them away); editing `METHOD.md`.

**Cost:** ~0 GPU time — a data-processing script over already-saved
JSON, plus bootstrap CIs (CPU, minutes at most). **State the measured
wall time in `R`** anyway, per standing project practice of always
reporting it, however small.
