### R1 — What the table shows (descriptive, per the D)

- **Ordering on every set: JointGreedy > H1-linear ≈ H1-attention > GreedyCover ≳

  hard-model advantage is not something H.1's real search finds and our
  classifier-free chain misses. It is the other way round, at the level of

- **H1-linear's higher all-37 score (+0.8 pp over H1-attention) does not
  reach the hard models.** It ties or trails H1-attention on 3 of 4 sets.
  Whatever the linear-guided search bought, it went to the easier models.
- **Consistent with D018/R5** (pair-restricted `hard_subset`, same ordering
  JointGreedy ≳ H1 ≳ GreedyCover). This is the model-restricted, true-37-way
  companion to that result.

**Resolution caveat, stated rather than tested (no test per the D).** The
restricted means rest on 6–21 models × 25 test configs. Their spread across

that repeats across four sets and two H1 chains. It is not a resolved
difference, and should not be written up as one.** Per-seed ranges for all
