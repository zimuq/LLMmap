### What is and is not known going in

- D018's `metrics_by_k.json` stores `mean_top1`/`worst_class`/
  `worst3_class`/`hard_subset` per chain/readout/`k`, **but no
  `per_model` array** — confirmed by inspection (2026-09-27). The
  restricted-recall analysis needs this and cannot be derived from what
  is already saved.
- D018/R6 discloses that the attention-network checkpoints for both
  chains' final 5-seed evaluations were **saved but not committed**
  (gitignored per D009's convention, left on `$WORK`). **If they still
  exist**, this is a cheap, read-only re-inference — no retraining —
  exactly D016's S0/S1 pattern (load checkpoint, one forward pass on
  `S_test`, extract `per_model`, assert the recovered `mean_top1`
  matches the already-stored value). **If they have been cleaned up**,
  retraining just these two chains at `k`=8, 5 seeds (attention) + 1
  deterministic fit (linear) is itself cheap (on the order of D009's
  original per-run cost, minutes total) — state which branch applies,
  do not silently assume either way.
- **The model sets to restrict to are already defined and must be reused
  exactly, not re-derived**, to stay consistent with the (still-parked)
  hard-set-definition discussion and avoid a second, subtly different
  version of the same table existing in the project:
