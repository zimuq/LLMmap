## Why this D

- **D038 (S_build only) located MMR\*'s v2 edge over JG.**
  - It is **query strength**: MMR\*'s queries are individually stronger, +0.017 in held-out s_q, 40 / 40, 4× the v1
    difference.
  - It is **not redundancy**: JG's set is the less redundant one.
  - It is **not noise-chasing**: optimism ≈ 0 for every rule.
  - Set-level tail yardsticks (JG's joint statistic, MAX coverage) rank sets almost opposite to accuracy. JG and GC
    both pick some of the pool's weakest single queries (JG: ranks 149 and 256; GC: 149, 256, 233, 258) because
    those fill specific bottom-decile pairs.
  - Pure strength (TOP-K[CVaR], effective number 3.0) loses to MMR\* on v2. So enough diversity is also needed.
- **MMR\* gets that balance through λ**, which D031 had to choose on S_val (pearson 0.7). The human asked for a rule
  that reaches it without a tuned trade-off parameter.
- **Why SUM is the candidate.** SUM-coverage scores a pair by the sum of its per-query energies, so every query's
  evidence counts.
  - Under MAX, a strong query that separates the same pairs as an earlier one adds zero (D038: GC skips query 0,
    rank 2, correlation 0.835 with 193). Under SUM it still adds. So strength is not discarded.
  - Under CVaR₀.₁ the gain is still scored on the current bottom decile, so a query that only repeats easy pairs
    adds little. That is the diversity pressure.
  - The trained and summed readouts both accumulate evidence across queries. D021 (v1) found accumulation ranks pair
    difficulty better than the joint statistic or MAX.
- **Why it might fail (stated before the data).** SUM + CVaR is still a set-level tail yardstick, the kind D038
  found misaligned with accuracy. It may also reach for weak tail specialists. Stage 1 checks this directly, before
  any training.
- **Prior evidence (v1 only).**
  - D023: an exact ILP maximising CVaR₀.₁ of summed, whitened evidence (`ADD`) tied JG at every `k`. On v1, though,
    JG, MMR\* and TOP-K also tie, so v1 cannot separate these rules.
  - D008 ran SUM only at γ = 1, where it reduces to ranking by mean (= TOP-K[MEAN]).
  - **SUM + CVaR has never been run.**
- **Why TH(τ) is added (2026-11-12, design-side audit).**
  - MMR's λ trades a pool-min–max-normalised strength against a correlation. The same λ is therefore a different
    trade-off on another pool. MMR's v2 range over its 6 variants is 7.7 pp (attention, `k` = 8).
  - TH keeps TOP-K's strength order and only vetoes near-duplicates. Its τ is in correlation units and is invariant to
    any monotone rescaling of strength.
  - D038 `full.json`: 5 of TOP-K's 7 later v2 picks have a correlation ≥ 0.7 with an earlier pick (MMR\*: 1 / 7).
    They are the same probe type: two injection wrappers and five harmful-request refusal probes.
- **Why Part B is added.** In D038 no set-level yardstick predicted accuracy. On the five D038 rules, mean s_q did
  better than Y_joint (Spearman 0.6–0.9 against 0.1–0.3 on v2, descriptive). Part B pre-registers a model that
  combines strength, redundancy and the tail without λ or γ, and tests it on every stored chain, not just 5.

## D — The question

> **On v2, does a λ-free rule (SUM-coverage + CVaR₀.₁, or TH(τ)) select a set that keeps individually strong queries
> (like MMR\*) while avoiding near-duplicates, and does it then match MMR\*'s accuracy? (Part B:) does an
> additive-evidence model of set quality predict the stored accuracy of existing chains better than mean strength and
> JG's Y_joint?**

### Arms

| arm | selection call |
|---|---|
| **SUM** | `LLMmap/greedy_cover.greedy_cover(E, 8, gamma=0.1, agg="sum")` on the frozen sf tensor (both views, full S_build). On D038's halves, `E` = the half's path-1 energies (`results/D038/energies_<view>.npz`). Unchanged code. |
| **TH(τ)** | New, about 10 lines in `experiments/d039_lib.py`; no LLMmap code is edited. (1) Rank the pool by `d031_lib.single_cvar(E)`, the TOP-K[CVaR] order with ties to the lower id. (2) Walk down the ranking and accept q if `max_{s ∈ selected} sim[q, s] < τ`, with `sim = d031_lib.similarity(E, "pearson")`, MMR\*'s similarity. (3) Stop at 8. If the pool runs out first, that τ is ineligible: report it and do not backfill. |

**TH's τ.**
- Grid τ ∈ {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9}; τ = 1 would be TOP-K.
- τ\* is chosen with D031's MMR variant-choice protocol, verbatim: linear readout (D017 concat) fit on S_build, top-1 on
  S_val at `k` = 8, a tie going to the larger τ.
- This is the same S_val use that chose MMR\*'s λ, so TH and MMR\* are tuned on equal terms.
- τ\* is chosen per view and committed before any Stage 1 reading.
- On D038's halves, TH is reselected with τ\* fixed.

- **`agg="sum"` is the module's documented isolation variant.** No code is edited. Running it as an arm does not
  change I4; any change to I4 would be a METHOD revision.
- **References (reused verbatim, nothing re-selected or re-trained):** JG, MMR\* (`MMR|pearson|0.7`),
  TOP-K[CVaR], GC (MAX) and PAPER8. Stage 1 uses D038's stored per-split chains and measures. Stage 2 uses the
  stored D029 / D031 / D032 / D037 counts.
- **SUM has no tunable parameter.** γ = 0.1 is the method default. S_val is used only for TH's τ.

### Part B — the additive-evidence model (no training; runs alongside Stage 1; both views)

**Model (pre-registered).**
- e_qp is the frozen full-S_build sf energy of query q on pair p, clipped at 0.
  - sf divides the energy distance by within-model spread, so for small shifts it behaves like a squared
    signal-to-noise ratio, and √e_qp like a signal-to-noise ratio.
- For a set S, the pair's combined evidence is computed under two readout assumptions:

| variant | combined evidence D_p(S) | assumption |
|---|---|---|
| **A_opt** | Σ_{q∈S} e_qp | independent per-query noise, each query weighted by its own strength (as a trained readout can learn). A weak query adds a little and never hurts. |
| **A_eq** | (Σ_{q∈S} √e_qp)² / \|S\| | independent noise, equal weights (as readout ② / ④). A weak query adds its noise in full and dilutes the evidence. |

- Set quality: A(S) = mean over pairs of F(D_p(S)), with F(D) = 1 − exp(−c·D).
  - **c₀** = ln 2 / median_p(Σ_{q∈PAPER8} e_qp): the scale at which PAPER8 saturates half the pairs. PAPER8 is not a
    candidate set, so this is neutral.
  - c₀ is computed once per view.
  - Robustness grid: c ∈ c₀ × {¼, ½, 1, 2, 4}.

**Chains (fixed list, P enumerates them).** Every chain with stored old-S_test E_all under the D029 / D031 protocol:
- D031's deterministic arms (JG, PAPER8, TOP-K, 6 MMR variants, HELM deterministic);
- GC (D029);
- TOP-K[MEAN] (D032);
- JG-mean and GC-mean (D037);
- the random and HELM draws (D031) as a secondary set.

No chain is retrained. These are published numbers, and no new S_test evaluation is made.

**Competing measures, each per chain at `k` = 8.**
- mean s_q;
- Y_joint (path 2) and Y_max;
- the SUM-coverage CVaR₀.₁ objective;
- A_opt and A_eq.

**Readings** (Spearman ρ across chains, per view; paired bootstrap over chains, 2000 resamples, for differences in ρ).

| | reading | what it tests |
|---|---|---|
| **B1 (primary)** | ρ(A_opt, E_all) against ρ(mean s_q, E_all) and ρ(Y_joint, E_all), attention and linear, at c₀ | **Supported** if, on v2, A_opt's ρ is highest under both trained readouts and the bootstrap band of ρ(A_opt) − ρ(mean s_q) under linear lies above 0. |
| **B2 (noise accumulation)** | ρ(A_eq, ④) − ρ(A_opt, ④), and ρ(A_opt, trained) − ρ(A_eq, trained) | Whether weak queries add noise under an equal-weight readout but not under a trained one. ④ = D038's `readout2` P_all at `k` = 8, median over the 40 directed splits, computed for every deterministic chain in the list (fixed chains, not reselected). **Supported** if both differences are > 0 on v2 with bands above 0. |
| B3 (descriptive) | noise correlation vs profile correlation | Within-set mean, per chain, of two quantities. **Noise correlation:** for query q, model v and S_build config c, let z_qvc = the Euclidean distance of c's response embedding to model v's centroid for q, standardised within (q, v); the noise correlation of q and q′ is the mean over models of the Pearson over configs of z_q·· and z_q′··. **Profile correlation:** M2's within-set Pearson. Does TOP-K's high profile correlation (.37) come with a high noise correlation? |
| B4 (pre-registered prediction) | A_opt rank of SUM's and TH(τ\*)'s chains, written in the Stage 1 gate commit | If Stage 2 runs, R checks the prediction against their trained E_all. |

- Robustness: B1 and B2 are also reported across the c grid. A reading that holds only at c₀ is labelled "c-fragile".
- Secondary: the pooled (chain, `k`) cells for `k` = 1..8, and the draw sets. Both are descriptive.
- **Pre-registered interpretation:**
  - **B1 supported:** additive accumulation with saturation is the working model of set quality. Design-side drafts
    a later D for greedy maximisation of A_opt as a selection rule. That rule would need no λ and no γ: saturation
    gives the de-duplication, and unsaturated pairs give the tail focus.
  - **B1 not supported:** the model is not used as a set measure. The "strength + de-duplication" framing proceeds
    with S_val-tuned TH or MMR.
  - **B2** is read whatever B1's outcome, as the test of whether noise accumulates in equal-weight readouts.

## Constraints

- **I1:** tail plots before readings, in both stages.
- **I2:**
  - Selection on S_build (or its halves) only.
  - S_val: TH's τ choice (D031 protocol) and attention early stopping only.
  - S_test: read once, and only in Stage 2.
  - Stage 1 reads no S_test, and no S_val beyond Sτ.
  - Part B reads stored old-S_test E_all values and makes no new S_test evaluation. It validates a measure and selects
    no arm.
- **I3:** every energy is cloud vs cloud (halves ≥ 37 configs).
- **I4:** SUM is run as an ablation arm with the module's isolation flag. The method default stays MAX until a
  METHOD revision is approved by the human. GC is the MAX reference.
- **I5 / I6:** frozen embeddings and tensors. D038's half energies are reused by sha. Reference counts are verbatim.
- **I7:** no corpus change.

**Out of scope:**
- other γ;
- SUM on the joint (accumulator) statistic;
- whitened / `ADD` evidence (D021 / D023);
- hybrid rules (e.g. a strength floor on JG);
- τ outside the grid;
- selecting by A_opt (a later D, if B1 is supported);
- fitting F or c to accuracy;
- v1 training;
- open-set;
- S_test′.

**Cost (design-side guess):**
- Stage 1: CPU, < 0.3 node-h (SUM selection is seconds; M4 as D038).
- Sτ: 5 linear fits per view, about 0.1 node-h.
- Part B: CPU. ④ for about 17 chains × 40 splits at `k` = 8, plus the noise correlation: < 0.5 node-h.
- Stage 2: up to 2 arms × 8 `k` × 5 seeds = 80 attention runs, plus 16 linear fits, ≈ 1.2 node-h (D037: 1.19 node-h
  for 2 arms).
- **Call if > 4 node-h.** State the measured cost in R.
