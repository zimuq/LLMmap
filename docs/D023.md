## D — The question

> **For each budget `k`=1..8, solve the exact integer program "choose `k`
> queries maximising CVaR₀.₁ over the 666 pairs of summed evidence `ADD`".
> Trained under the standing protocol, is that chain at least as accurate
> as JointGreedy (γ=0.1)? Separately, how close to the exact optimum are
> the greedy chains we already have, in `ADD` units?**

### Why this is worth doing, precisely

D021 showed that `ADD` is a better *ruler*. This D asks whether a better
ruler gives a better *choice*. The answer is not guaranteed.

- **Exact optimisation of an over-confident proxy is where Goodhart
  failures appear.** D021 found `ADD`'s probabilities are about 100× too
  optimistic, and an ILP searches the proxy's blind spots more
  aggressively than greedy does.
- **Winner's curse is this project's recurring failure** (D011: an
  argmax over 259 noisy per-query scores scored worse than random). An
  exact argmax over subsets is the same risk, at a larger scale.

Two arms separate the two things that change at once:

| arm | objective | optimiser | isolates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`ILP-ADD`** (primary) | CVaR₀.₁ of `ADD` | exact MILP, per `k` | — |
| **`Greedy-ADD`** (secondary) | CVaR₀.₁ of `ADD` | greedy, nested chain | Greedy-ADD vs JointGreedy = **objective** effect; ILP-ADD vs Greedy-ADD = **optimiser** effect |

**The LP certificate is a deliverable in its own right.**
- It answers a question this project has never been able to answer:
  *how far from optimal are our greedy chains?*
- It is measured in `ADD` units only. D021 showed those units are not
  probabilities, so it certifies proxy optimality, not accuracy.

### The optimisation — fixed here

- **Evidence.** D021's primary tensor `M²_q(p)`: 259 × 666, d=64, full
  S_build `Σ_q`, D007 halves, signed. No other variant, including D021's
  per-pair-Σ variant, which is no longer blind to D021's targets.
- **Pair evidence.** `A_x(p) = Σ_q x_q M²_q(p)`, with `x_q ∈ {0,1}` and
  `Σ_q x_q = k`.
- **Objective.** Maximise the sum of the `m` smallest `A_x(p)`, with
  `m = floor(0.1 × 666) = 66`. This is the exact definition used by
  `cvar()` in `LLMmap/joint_statistic.py`.
- **LP form** (exact for integer `m`):
  - maximise `m·t − Σ_p u_p`
  - subject to `u_p ≥ t − A_x(p)`, `u_p ≥ 0`, `Σ x_q = k`, `x ∈ {0,1}^259`.
  - The optimal value divided by `m` is CVaR₀.₁.
- **Solver.** Any open MILP solver (e.g. HiGHS via `scipy.optimize.milp`,
  or CBC). P names it, with deterministic settings.
  - Wall-clock limit: 15 min per `k`.
  - If optimality is not proven, report the incumbent and the final MIP
    gap. The chain is still used; the gap is disclosed.
  - Report whether multiple optima exist, if the solver can tell.
- **Non-nesting.** Each `k` is solved independently, so `ILP-ADD` sets
  need not be nested. Report the nesting pattern; do not force nesting.
- **Greedy-ADD.** At each step add the query with the largest gain in
  the same objective. Ties go to the lowest query id.
- **Certificate.**
  - For every `k`, solve the LP relaxation (`x ∈ [0,1]`) → upper bound
    `UB_k`.
  - Report each chain's objective as a fraction of `UB_k`, and of the ILP
    optimum. Chains: ILP-ADD, Greedy-ADD, JointGreedy, GreedyCover,
    paper8, H1-attention, H1-linear.

### What is and is not known going in

- JointGreedy's trained numbers (attention, 5 seeds; linear) and its
  666-pair `k`=8 values exist (D009/D010/D016/D020). Nothing needs
  rerunning (I6).
- `k`=1: the ILP optimum is the single query with the highest CVaR of
  `M²_q`. This need not be JointGreedy's `{193}`, so `k`=1 is a real
  comparison here, unlike D010/D015's identical-input `k`=1.
- Exploratory label: the same 25-config S_test again. If this D comes
  back BETTER or PARITY, freeze its chain for the fresh-S_test
  confirmatory D. Do not treat this D's number as the confirmation.

## What counts as an answer

**Primary endpoint.**
- The quantity is the **mean over `k`=1..8** of Δ mean top-1
  (`ILP-ADD` − JointGreedy), i.e. the area between the two k-curves. That
  matches A4's query-efficiency claim, and it is one number instead of
  eight correlated ones.
- The primary readout is the **attention network** (the paper's
  pipeline), with **hierarchical bootstrap** intervals:
  - resample the 25 test configs, paired across arms;
  - independently resample each arm's 5 seeds with replacement;
  - 2,000 draws, seed 20260928.
- **Why not the D009/F1 "CI excludes 0 and |Δ| > seed range" rule?** It
  compares a difference of 5-seed *means* against the range of *single*
  runs, and it has made nearly every attention comparison unresolvable
  since D009 (design-side audit, 2026-09-28). The hierarchical bootstrap
  carries both uncertainty sources at the right scale.
  - **Also report the old rule's reading** for continuity with D009–D020.
    It is a disclosure, not the verdict.

**Pre-registered outcomes** (95% interval of the primary quantity; δ =
0.02, the project's TOST margin on accuracy):

| outcome | criterion |
|---|---|
| **BETTER** | lower bound > 0 |
| **PARITY** | interval within [−δ, +δ] |
| **WORSE** | upper bound < 0 |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | anything else |

**Secondary endpoints** (reported per `k` and under the same bootstrap;
not part of the verdict):

- (S-a) **Linear readouts** (concat, mean-pool), same quantity.
  Deterministic fits, so config bootstrap only (D020's convention).
- (S-b) **Tail, the objective's own target.** At `k`=8: CVaR₀.₁ over the
  666 pairs of trained two-logit accuracy (D016's extraction). Compare
  `ILP-ADD` and `Greedy-ADD` against JointGreedy and paper8 (D016 has
  their values). Also report the worst pair and the two named pairs.
  - This is the direct test of "hard pairs improve".
  - Requires saving 666-pair per-config counts for the new arms (all 5
    seeds).
- (S-c) **Goodhart diagnostic.** For each new chain at `k`=8:
  - ρ(`ADD`, trained 666-pair accuracy), same footing as D021;
  - tail agreement: overlap of the worst `m`=66 pairs by `ADD` vs by
    trained accuracy, next to the same numbers for JointGreedy's chain.
  - **Flag, don't verdict,** if the new chain's ρ is more than 0.15
    (about one D021 interval width) below D021's pooled 0.595.
- (S-d) The **objective and optimiser decomposition** (table above), on
  the primary quantity.
- (S-e) The **certificate table**, the solver gaps, the selection wall
  time, and chain overlaps with JointGreedy and paper8.

**Certificate preview at `k`=8** (fraction of the ILP optimum): JointGreedy
**92.3%**, H1-linear 73.7%, paper8 66.5%, GreedyCover 66.1%, H1-attention
64.1%. The optimum is unique but **flat**, which is the winner's-curse setting
the D warns about.

**Call 1 — seeds resampled jointly across arms, not independently.** At
identical-input `k`, the two arms' runs are bit-identical per seed (seeded
training is deterministic). Independent resampling would manufacture variance
where Δ ≡ 0, and it overstates variance whenever the seed-*r* runs are
correlated. Resampling seed *r* jointly is valid either way and exact here. The
full scheme is written out in the plan:

- configs: `boot_draws(25, 2000, seed=20260928)`, shared by both arms and all
  `k`;
- seed indices: shared by both arms, independent across `k`;
- 5-with-replacement understates seed variance by ×4/5, disclosed.

**Call 2 — identical `k` dilute PARITY only.** Under Call 1, identical-input
`k` add exact zeros. The mean over `k`=1..8 then rescales both the point and the
interval by *n_diff*/8.

- BETTER and WORSE are unchanged, because they depend only on a bound's sign.
- PARITY gets easier: with 2 of 8 identical, it effectively tests ±0.027.
- **Recommend:** BETTER/WORSE on the D's quantity, and PARITY on the mean over
  differing `k`. That list is fixed at S1 from selection alone, before training.

**Call 3 (minor) — ordering ILP sets.** JointGreedy's order for shared queries
first, then ascending id. Identical-set `k` then become literal identical
inputs, and S2 asserts that they reproduce D010/D020's counts exactly.

**Cost:** S1 about 1 min CPU; S2 about 20 GPU-min (80 attention runs + reload
for 666-pair counts, 32 linear fits); S3 minutes.

  value narrows the interval.
