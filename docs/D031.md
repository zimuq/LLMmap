## Why this D

- Our paper's central claim is about **choosing which queries to send**.
- D030 compared whole pipelines. This D holds the readout fixed and
  compares **only the query-selection rule**, the D018 / D023 / D024 shape.
- **Helm et al. 2026** is the closest published method.
  - It represents a black-box model by **per-query energy distances between
    response distributions**: d_Q² = mean over queries of d_P², which is
    close to our CLOUD / ADD statistics.
  - It selects queries from the **SVD of the query × model-pair matrix**.
- **Their setting differs from ours, so the method must be adapted:**
  - their task is binary *property* classification over many labelled
    systems; ours is 85-way *identity* classification;
  - their estimated rank was r̂ = 1 in all three tasks;
  - their selection is a filter: keep the "estimated signal" queries, then
    sample m uniformly from them. Their own limitation (iii) says ranking
    within the signal set is future work.
- The adaptation below is the most faithful reading. It is disclosed as an
  adaptation, plus one labelled extension.

## D — The question

> **With the readout and the candidate pool fixed (corpus v2's 259 queries,
> v1's 259 for the v1 view), does JointGreedy-CVaR's 8-query set train to
> better identification than the sets chosen by Helm et al.'s spectral rule,
> by individually-strongest top-k, by MMR, and by random draws?**
>
> **Secondary: at a matched target-side budget, how does MET compare with
> our method?**

### Selection arms (all on S_build only; chains frozen and committed before any training)

| arm | rule | deterministic? |
|---|---|---|
| **JG** | D029's v2 chain / D010's v1 chain, reused verbatim | yes |
| **PAPER8** | LLMmap's 8 queries (reference row) | yes |
| **HELM-SIG** *(faithful)* | (1) Build E (queries × model pairs) of per-query **raw squared energy distance** between the two models' S_build point clouds (Helm's d_P²; the unordered 3,570 / 666 pairs, which have the same left singular vectors as their n² ordered pairs). (2) Plain SVD of E, no centring. r̂ = argmax_s σ_s / σ_{s+1}. (3) For each ℓ ≤ r̂: fit a GMM, K ∈ {1, 2} by BIC, to {\|U_{jℓ}\|}. If K = 2, the zero-set is the component nearer 0; if K = 1, the zero-set is empty. (4) Estimated Signal = queries outside the intersection of the zero-sets. (5) **Sample k uniformly** from Estimated Signal, D draws | no (D draws) |
| **HELM-RANK** *(labelled extension of their limitation iii)* | Steps (1)–(2), then rank queries by the norm of their rank-r̂ row, ‖(U Σ)_{j, 1:r̂}‖, and take the top k | yes |
| **TOP-K** | rank queries by JG's own objective on one query (CVaR₀.₁ over pairs of the scale-free energy, the frozen tensor) and take the top k. No interaction between queries; isolates JG's set-level greedy | yes |
| **MMR** | greedy: add argmax of λ · rel(q) − (1 − λ) · max_{s ∈ S} sim(q, s), with rel = TOP-K's single-query score (min–max scaled over the pool) and sim = cosine similarity between two queries' 3,570-pair (666) profiles in the frozen tensor. λ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7}, one chosen on S_val (below) | yes |
| **RANDOM** | k uniform from the 259-query pool, D draws | no (D draws) |

- **Variants and the strongest-variant rule (D030 lessons B2 / B3).**
  - HELM has 4 pre-declared variants: {SIG, RANK} × {E from raw energy
    (faithful), E from the frozen scale-free tensor (ours)}.
  - MMR has 3 (λ).
  - **Each method's comparison variant is chosen on S_val, not S_test.**
    Pick the highest linear-readout E_all at `k` = 8 on S_val; SIG uses the
    mean over its draws. This removes D030's B* optimism.
  - All variants are trained and reported.
- **Degenerate case, pre-registered.** If Estimated Signal is the whole pool
  (every direction K = 1), HELM-SIG ≡ RANDOM, and R says so. That is itself
  a finding: the filter does not discriminate in N-way identity
  classification. HELM-RANK still runs.
- **Descriptive: Helm's own ordering check in our setting.** Estimated
  Signal vs Estimated Orthogonal vs uniform pool, D draws each,
  `k` ∈ {2, 4, 8}, linear only. Also report r̂, the spectrum, the GMM fits and
  ρ̂_ℓ. "What is the discriminative rank of LLM identification?" is
  interesting in its own right.

## What counts as an answer

**Endpoints** (S_test, read once; `k` = 8; per view):
- **E_all:** mean top-1 (85-way on v2, 37-way on v1).
- **E_tail:** CVaR₀.₁ of two-logit accuracy over **all** pairs (3,570 /
  666). This is the unselected hard endpoint required by D030's lesson B1.
- **Secondary, not used for labels:** H_all mean (v2, D029), D019 hard-model
  sets (v1).

**Primary contrasts:** Δ = JG − X at `k` = 8, for X ∈ {HELM*, TOP-K, MMR*,
RANDOM}. Each is a separate question; there is no multiplicity correction
(D017–D024 convention).
- Interval: D023's hierarchical config × seed bootstrap, with D029's draws
  (v2) and D023's draws (v1). One draw set per view is shared by every arm.

| label (per X × view) | rule |
|---|---|
| **BETTER** | lo(Δ_all) > 0 **and** lo(Δ_tail) > 0, linear Δ agreeing in sign (point) on both |
| **PARITY** | both intervals ⊂ [−0.02, +0.02] |
| **WORSE** *(headline)* | hi(Δ_all) < 0 **or** hi(Δ_tail) < 0 |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise |

- Precedence: WORSE > BETTER > PARITY. A WORSE in any cell, either view,
  goes first in R and in the Review's "For you".
- **The v2 view is primary; v1 is reported with equal prominence.** The two
  are never compared as levels (D029 rule 2).

**Secondary (descriptive):**
- (a) Curves over `k` for every arm and both readouts. Also the `k` at which
  each arm first reaches JG's `k` = 4 and `k` = 8 E_all ("queries to
  reach").
- (b) Overlap of each chain with JG and PAPER8; D022 union-flag counts.
- (c) Helm diagnostics (above), and the ordering check.
- (d) The mandatory hard-tail plot, all pairs, every arm at `k` = 8 (I1).

### Budget section — MET at matched target-side budget (CPU; D030's data, no generation)

**Why:** in D030, MET used 250 target-side completions against our 8.

**Subsampled target sets** (the reference side stays at its full 250; the
budget question is the cost per identification):

| n | prompts × samples per prompt |
|---|---|
| 8 | 8 × 1 |
| 25 | 25 × 1 |
| 50 | 25 × 2 |
| 100 | 25 × 4 |
| 250 | 25 × 10 (= D030) |

- Prompts (when 8 < 25) and samples are drawn uniformly, with 20 draws per
  n, seeded as above.
- **Score with fewer than 2 samples per prompt.** The target-target term of
  the prompt-aware MMD is the same for every candidate, so it drops out of
  the ranking. Use score_m = 2 · mean k(target, ref_m) − mean k(ref_m, ref_m)
  over within-prompt pairs. Code-path check: at n = 250 it must give the
  same ranking as D030's −MMD²_u (exact top-1 agreement on every target).
- **Cells:** MET-Hamming and MET-e5, each with the Gen and All libraries;
  both views; E_all and E_tail.
- **Our curve:** D029 JG `k` = 1–8, 12, 16 (v2) and D010 / D025 (v1), as
  stored.
- **Matched-budget label (pre-registered):** at n = k = 8, Δ = JG(`k` = 8,
  attention) − MET*(n = 8). MET* is the strongest of its 4 cells, chosen
  by D030's S_test E_all at n = 250 (the only selection available
  without S_val generations; this favours MET). Same label table as above.
- Also report the **smallest n at which MET reaches JG's `k` = 8 E_all**,
  if any.
- **Honesty note for R:** D030 already read these S_test data at n = 250.
  The n < 250 values are new, and their rule is fixed here before they are
  computed.

## Required steps

**S0 — Selection (CPU). No training.**
- Build E:
  - raw squared energy from the frozen embeddings with
    `LLMmap.joint_statistic`;
  - assert raw / within = the frozen scale-free tensor, ≤ 1e-6, on 3
    queries × 50 pairs.
- Run the SVD, r̂ and the GMMs. Then build every chain and draw: JG
  (reproduce D029 / D010 exactly as a code-path check), HELM-SIG / RANK × 2
  E-variants, TOP-K, MMR × 3 λ, RANDOM, and the ordering-check sets.
- **Freeze, commit and push `chains.json`** (incl. all draw seeds) **before
  any training.**

**S1 — S_val variant choice (linear only).**
- Fit on S_build and score S_val for HELM's 4 variants and MMR's 3 λ at
  `k` = 8.
- Pick HELM* and MMR*. Commit `variant_choice.json` before any S_test read.

**S2 — Train.**
- Every arm × `k` × readout as specified.
- Counts in D029's format (`cnt_model`, `cnt_pair`, `dirH`, plus all-pair
  arrays for E_tail).
- Exact-reproduction asserts on any arm × `k` whose query set equals JG's or
  PAPER8's (D023 Call 2 canonical order).

**S3 — Budget section scoring** (CPU / GPU, D030's derived data): the
code-path check first, then the subsampled scores and counts.

**S4 — Analysis:** WORSE scan → labels → secondary → budget label → tail
plot.

**S5 — `## R`:** labels first; every table names its view.

**Multi-P:** a single P is expected. There is one free-window stop: chains
and variant choice are committed before any S_test read. TACC may split per
D030's multi-P rules if useful.

**Ordering (human, 2026-10-12): quick results are welcome, without
reading early.**
- Helm needs **no generation**. Its E matrix is built from the frozen
  S_build embeddings (CPU), and its arms train like any other.
- TACC may therefore start training the generic arms (TOP-K, MMR, RANDOM)
  while Helm's E, SVD and GMM are still being built.
- **No arm's S_test number is computed or read until every chain, Helm's
  included, and `variant_choice.json` are committed.** That keeps Helm's
  adaptation blind to the other arms' results. S_val reads for the variant
  choice are allowed as soon as the relevant chains exist.

## Constraints

- **I2:** selection on S_build; variant choice on S_val; S_test read once,
  in S4.
- **I3:** every statistic is per-model point clouds (Helm's d_P² is energy
  between config-marginal clouds).
- **I5 / I6:** the frozen embeddings and tensors only. The raw energy for E
  is recomputed from the same embeddings under the assert. JG / PAPER8
  counts are reused verbatim.
- **I4:** untouched. **I7:** no corpus change.
- **No tuning beyond the pre-declared variants.**
  - If Helm's GMM or rank step behaves degenerately, that is reported (see
    the degenerate case above), not patched.
  - If P sees a reason the adaptation is unfair to Helm et al., raise a
    Call. **Don't build a strawman** (D024 / D030).
- **Out of scope:**
  - Helm's own readout (MDS + random forest over instances) and their
    instance-level framing; the readout is held fixed here;
  - module-level MET arms;
  - open-set (separate D, TODO T6.4);
  - any new generation.
- **Cost (design-side guess):**
  - selection: CPU minutes, plus the raw-E build;
  - attention ≈ 5 deterministic arms (incl. MMR*, HELM-RANK*) × 8 `k` × 5
    seeds + 2 draw arms × 10 draws × 5 seeds, ≈ 300 runs per view;
  - linear: all variants and draws;
  - budget section: about 1 node-h.
  - Expected ≲ 15 node-h. **Call if > 40.**
