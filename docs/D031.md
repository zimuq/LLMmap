## Why this D

- Our paper's central claim is about **choosing which queries to send**.
- D030 compared whole pipelines. This D holds the readout fixed and
  compares **only the query-selection rule**, the D018 / D023 / D024 shape.
- **Helm et al. 2026** is the closest published method.
  - It represents a black-box model by **per-query energy distances between
    response distributions**: d_Q² = mean over queries of d_P², which is
    close to our CLOUD / ADD statistics.
  - It selects queries from the **SVD of the query × model-pair matrix**.
- **Their setting differs from ours, so the method must be adapted:**
  - their task is binary *property* classification over many labelled
    systems; ours is 85-way *identity* classification;
  - their estimated rank was r̂ = 1 in all three tasks;
  - their selection is a filter: keep the "estimated signal" queries, then
    sample m uniformly from them. Their own limitation (iii) says ranking
    within the signal set is future work.
- The adaptation below is the most faithful reading. It is disclosed as an
  adaptation, plus one labelled extension.

## D — The question

> **With the readout and the candidate pool fixed (corpus v2's 259 queries,
> v1's 259 for the v1 view), does JointGreedy-CVaR's 8-query set train to
> better identification than the sets chosen by Helm et al.'s spectral rule,
> by individually-strongest top-k, by MMR, and by random draws?**
>
> **Secondary: at a matched target-side budget, how does MET compare with
> our method?**

### Selection arms (all on S_build only; chains frozen and committed before any training)

| arm | rule | deterministic? |
|---|---|---|
| **JG** | D029's v2 chain / D010's v1 chain, reused verbatim | yes |
| **PAPER8** | LLMmap's 8 queries (reference row) | yes |
| **HELM-SIG** *(faithful)* | (1) Build E (queries × model pairs) of per-query **raw squared energy distance** between the two models' S_build point clouds (Helm's d_P²; the unordered 3,570 / 666 pairs, which have the same left singular vectors as their n² ordered pairs). (2) Plain SVD of E, no centring. r̂ = argmax_s σ_s / σ_{s+1}. (3) For each ℓ ≤ r̂: fit a GMM, K ∈ {1, 2} by BIC, to {\|U_{jℓ}\|}. If K = 2, the zero-set is the component nearer 0; if K = 1, the zero-set is empty. (4) Estimated Signal = queries outside the intersection of the zero-sets. (5) **Sample k uniformly** from Estimated Signal, D draws | no (D draws) |
| **HELM-RANK** *(labelled extension of their limitation iii)* | Steps (1)–(2), then rank queries by the norm of their rank-r̂ row, ‖(U Σ)_{j, 1:r̂}‖, and take the top k | yes |
| **TOP-K** | rank queries by JG's own objective on one query (CVaR₀.₁ over pairs of the scale-free energy, the frozen tensor) and take the top k. No interaction between queries; isolates JG's set-level greedy | yes |
| **MMR** | greedy: add argmax of λ · rel(q) − (1 − λ) · max_{s ∈ S} sim(q, s), with rel = TOP-K's single-query score (min–max scaled over the pool) and sim = cosine similarity between two queries' 3,570-pair (666) profiles in the frozen tensor. λ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7}, one chosen on S_val (below) | yes |
| **RANDOM** | k uniform from the 259-query pool, D draws | no (D draws) |

### Budget section — MET at matched target-side budget (CPU; D030's data, no generation)

**Why:** in D030, MET used 250 target-side completions against our 8.

**Subsampled target sets** (the reference side stays at its full 250; the
budget question is the cost per identification):

| n | prompts × samples per prompt |
|---|---|
| 8 | 8 × 1 |
| 25 | 25 × 1 |
| 50 | 25 × 2 |
| 100 | 25 × 4 |
| 250 | 25 × 10 (= D030) |

- Prompts (when 8 < 25) and samples are drawn uniformly, with 20 draws per
  n, seeded as above.
- **Score with fewer than 2 samples per prompt.** The target-target term of
  the prompt-aware MMD is the same for every candidate, so it drops out of
  the ranking. Use score_m = 2 · mean k(target, ref_m) − mean k(ref_m, ref_m)
  over within-prompt pairs. Code-path check: at n = 250 it must give the
  same ranking as D030's −MMD²_u (exact top-1 agreement on every target).
- **Cells:** MET-Hamming and MET-e5, each with the Gen and All libraries;
  both views; E_all and E_tail.
- **Our curve:** D029 JG `k` = 1–8, 12, 16 (v2) and D010 / D025 (v1), as
  stored.
- **Matched-budget label (pre-registered):** at n = k = 8, Δ = JG(`k` = 8,
  attention) − MET*(n = 8). MET* is the strongest of its 4 cells, chosen
  by D030's S_test E_all at n = 250 (the only selection available
  without S_val generations; this favours MET). Same label table as above.
- Also report the **smallest n at which MET reaches JG's `k` = 8 E_all**,
  if any.
- **Honesty note for R:** D030 already read these S_test data at n = 250.
  The n < 250 values are new, and their rule is fixed here before they are

**Call 1 — approved.** E_raw is the frozen raw tensor (`S_energy_raw_tok200[_v2]`, built in the same

- **Design-side expectation, recorded before S0 (descriptive, not a rule):**
  - E is entrywise non-negative with a strong common component. So σ₁/σ₂ is likely the largest
    ratio and r̂ = 1, as in all three of Helm's tasks.
  - At r̂ = 1, u₁ has one sign (Perron–Frobenius, as P notes), so HELM-RANK ranks queries by their
    loading on the dominant "overall separation" direction. That is close to ranking by a weighted
    **mean** separation over pairs.
  - TOP-K ranks by the **tail** (CVaR₀.₁). HELM-RANK vs TOP-K would then mostly contrast a mean
    criterion with a tail criterion on single queries, which echoes D008's mean-greedy vs CVaR.
  - R reports which happened. If r̂ = 1, R says this plainly and does not over-read HELM as a
    different idea.

**Call 3 — approved.**

**Call 4 — amended (A1): add centred similarity as a pre-declared MMR variant.**
- TACC's diagnosis is right. The rows of the scale-free tensor are non-negative and share the
  dominant direction, so uncentred cosine sits near 1 for most query pairs. The diversity term then
  varies little, and MMR degenerates toward TOP-K at every λ. That is a strawman by construction (the
  D's "don't build a strawman", D024 / D030 lesson B3).
- **MMR variants = {cosine, centred (Pearson) correlation of the pair profiles} × λ ∈ {0.3, 0.5,
  0.7} = 6.**
- MMR* is chosen on S_val linear at `k` = 8, as for the others. Ties go to cosine (the D's original),
  then the lower λ.
- Attention runs for all 6 (Call 6 logic). The extra cost is ≈ 120 v2 runs at ≈ 10 s, i.e. minutes.
- R reports the sim distributions for both measures and each variant's overlap with TOP-K.
- This is a pre-data amendment: no spectrum, chain or score exists yet.
