## Why this D

- In D031, **MMR\*** (relevance = single-query CVaR₀.₁, redundancy = centred
  Pearson between two queries' pair-profiles) beat JointGreedy on v2
  (E_all −0.058 attention) and tied it on v1. TOP-K (CVaR ranking, no
  redundancy term) was INCONCLUSIVE against JG in both views.
- MMR (Carbonell & Goldstein 1998) and mRMR (Peng et al. 2005) are not ours.
  If MMR becomes the headline rule, the paper's own contribution can only be
  **(a) the tail criterion** on config-marginal point clouds and **(b) the
  redundancy space** (pair-profiles rather than query text). D031 tests
  neither: it has no mean-criterion arm and no text-redundancy arm.
- **CVaR vs mean already exists for JG and GreedyCover** (D012, D008; v1
  only), but JG's mean objective is degenerate past `k` = 1 (D012/F1: the
  mean joint statistic falls with every added query), so that contrast mixes
  "tail helps" with "mean JG is broken". TOP-K and MMR have a well-behaved
  mean version, so the criterion contrast is clean there. **JG is not re-run
  here.**

## D — The question

> **With the fingerprint (scale-free energy between config-marginal point
> clouds, frozen tensor), the candidate pool (259 queries) and the readouts
> fixed:**
>
> **Q1 (criterion).** Does ranking queries by the **CVaR₀.₁ over pairs**
> train to better identification than ranking by the **mean over pairs**,
> under each of TOP-K, MMR-PP and MMR-TXT?
>
> **Q2 (redundancy space).** Does MMR's redundancy computed on
> **pair-profiles** beat the standard redundancy on **query-text
> embeddings**, with the criterion fixed?
>
> **Q3 (redundancy term).** With the criterion fixed, does adding a
> redundancy term beat plain ranking (MMR − TOP-K)?

### Selection arms (all on S_build; chains frozen and committed before any training)

Single-query score of query q, criterion c, computed from the frozen
scale-free tensor S[q, pair] over **all** pairs (3,570 v2 / 666 v1):
- **CVaR:** CVaR₀.₁ over pairs of S[q, ·] — exactly D031 TOP-K's score.
- **MEAN:** mean over pairs of S[q, ·] (the γ = 1 case of the same
  statistic, METHOD §5.2).

| arm | rule | new? |
|---|---|---|
| **TOP-K[c]** | top `k` by the single-query score under c | CVaR: reuse D031 TOP-K verbatim; MEAN: new |
| **MMR-PP[c]** | D031's MMR exactly: greedy argmax λ · rel_c(q) − (1 − λ) · max_{s ∈ S} sim(q, s); rel_c = the c-score min–max scaled over the pool; sim on the queries' pair-profiles S[q, ·] (**PP**). Variants: sim ∈ {cos, pearson (centred)} × λ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7} | CVaR: reuse D031's 6 MMR chains and its S_val choice (pearson, 0.7, both views) verbatim; MEAN: new |
| **MMR-TXT[c]** | same greedy, but sim on the **query-text embeddings** E(q) — the cached I5 embeddings the readout already uses (METHOD §5.5). Variants: sim ∈ {cos, centred cos (subtract the pool-mean E(q))} × λ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7}. This is MMR as used in IR | new, both c |

- **Reference rows (no new training, D031 counts as stored):** JG, PAPER8,
  GreedyCover (γ = 0.1), RANDOM.
- **Strongest-variant rule (D030 lessons B2 / B3, D031 convention).** Each
  MMR cell's starred variant is chosen on **S_val**: highest linear-readout
  E_all at `k` = 8. MMR-PP[CVaR] keeps D031's choice (same grid, same rule).
  All variants are trained and reported.
- **Degenerate cases, pre-registered.** If TOP-K[MEAN] or MMR-*[MEAN]
  selects the same 8 queries as its CVaR counterpart, Δ = 0 exactly; R
  says so ("the criterion does not change the set under this rule") and the
  cell counts as PARITY. Report the overlap of every CVaR / MEAN pair of
  chains regardless.

### Readouts and grid

- Readouts as D031: **attention** (D009 protocol, seeds 0–4) is the label
  readout; **linear** (D017 concat) is always reported, must agree in sign
  for BETTER, and gets its own "linear-as-label" column.
- `k` = 1–8 for every new arm (starred variants); non-starred variants at
  `k` = 8 only (attention) and `k` = 1–8 (linear).
- **Linear BLAS threads (D031 R §7 item 1):** match D031's stored counts —
  v2 linear at 8 threads, v1 linear at 16 threads — so reused and new arms
  are fitted under the same conditions.

## What counts as an answer

**Endpoints** (S_test, `k` = 8, per view): **E_all** (top-1; 85-way v2,
37-way v1) and **E_tail** (CVaR₀.₁ of two-logit accuracy over **all** pairs;
unselected, D030 lesson B1). Bootstrap: D023's hierarchical config × seed
scheme, 2,000 replicates, the same draw set per view as D031 (D029 v2, D023
v1). Labels exactly as D031:

| label | rule |
|---|---|
| **BETTER** | lo(Δ_all) > 0 **and** lo(Δ_tail) > 0, linear Δ agreeing in sign (point) on both |
| **PARITY** | both intervals ⊂ [−0.02, +0.02] |
| **WORSE** *(headline)* | hi(Δ_all) < 0 **or** hi(Δ_tail) < 0 |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise |

**Primary contrasts and claim rules (fixed here, before any new S_test
number):**

| Q | contrasts (each view) | claim SUPPORTED if | otherwise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1 criterion** | Δ = R[CVaR] − R[MEAN] for R ∈ {TOP-K, MMR-PP\*, MMR-TXT\*} | BETTER in ≥ 2 of 3 rules **in each view**, and no WORSE anywhere | ≥ 2 of 3 in one view only → SUPPORTED-v2 / SUPPORTED-v1; any WORSE → headline; else NOT SUPPORTED |
| **Q2 space** | Δ = MMR-PP\*[c] − MMR-TXT\*[c], c ∈ {CVaR, MEAN}; **c = CVaR is primary** | BETTER at c = CVaR in both views | one view → SUPPORTED-in-view; PARITY in both → "space does not matter" (a finding); WORSE → headline |
| **Q3 term** | Δ = MMR-X\*[c] − TOP-K[c], X ∈ {PP, TXT}, c ∈ {CVaR, MEAN} | reported per cell with labels; no aggregate claim | — |

- No multiplicity correction (D017–D031 convention); every cell is reported.
- **v2 is primary; v1 has equal prominence**; levels are never compared
  across views (D029 rule 2).

**Honesty note for R (required).** D031 already read S_test for
TOP-K[CVaR] and MMR-PP[CVaR], and this D was designed after seeing them
(D031 found MMR-PP[CVaR] > JG on v2). Every MEAN and TXT cell is a fresh
S_test read under the rules above. The Q3 cell MMR-PP[CVaR] − TOP-K[CVaR]
duplicates D031 S6 and is marked "seen". Confirmatory evidence for any
method switch comes only from the later fresh-S_test D.

**Secondary (descriptive):**
- (a) Curves over `k`, both readouts, all arms incl. reference rows;
  "queries to reach" JG's `k` = 4 / 8 E_all.
- (b) Chain overlaps: each arm vs JG, PAPER8, and its own CVaR / MEAN
  counterpart; D022 union-flag counts per chain.
- (c) **Mechanism:** for each CVaR / MEAN pair, the per-pair accuracy gain
  split by near-relative vs other pairs (the near-relative list per view: P
  names the file; if v2 has none, v1 only), and the share of near-relative
  pairs in each arm's worst decile.
- (d) Mean pairwise PP-similarity and TXT-similarity within each selected
  set (does MMR-TXT actually diversify pair-profiles, and vice versa?).
- (e) The mandatory hard-tail plot, all pairs, every arm at `k` = 8 (I1).
