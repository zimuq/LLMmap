## D — The question

> **Are some model pairs substantially harder to separate than others, and is that
> hardness *structured* — concentrated in fine-tune / same-family relationships —
> rather than spread uniformly across pairs?**

### Why this gates everything

CDQD rests on two premises (`METHOD.md` §2):

1. **A heavy hard tail exists.** If all pairs are comparably separable, then
   `CVaR_γ` is numerically equal to the mean, our objective is identical to
   LLMmap's, and every downstream comparison is a null experiment **that produces
   no error and no warning** (`METHOD.md` §6.4).
2. **The hard tail is structured.** Targeted generation (`METHOD.md` §5.4 step D)
   conditions a generator on a *confusion cluster*. If hard pairs are unstructured
   noise, there is no coherent cluster to condition on, and step D has no target.

Premise 1 alone justifies a new objective. Premise 1 **and** 2 justify the full
loop. If neither holds, we should know that now — before spending ~10⁵ generations
on a corpus.

### Prior expectation (state it now, so we can be wrong on record)

The paper's own worst case is Meta-Llama-3-70B-Instruct at 84%, and the confusion
matrix attributes it to Smaug-Llama-3-70B-Instruct — *a fine-tune of that model*.
We expect this to be an instance of a general pattern, not an isolated case.

**We are pre-registering this expectation deliberately.** If the data contradicts
it, that is a finding, not a failure.

---

## What counts as an answer

| Outcome | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **CONFIRMS** | The pairwise separability distribution has a distinct left tail, **and** the bottom decile is enriched in structurally-related pairs (base↔fine-tune, adjacent versions, same family) relative to their base rate among all pairs. |
| **FALSIFIES** | The bottom decile is statistically indistinguishable from a random draw over all pairs — i.e. hardness carries no structural signal. |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | Too few structurally-related pairs exist among the shipped models to detect enrichment, or the enrichment estimate's confidence interval spans "no effect". → report; do not force a verdict. |

**Report the actual distribution regardless of verdict.** The shape of that
distribution is itself an input to choosing `γ` (C1) and `θ` (C5).

## Required measurements

**M1 — Pairwise separability matrix** over all models shipped with the released
pretrained open-set model (`./data/pretrained_models/default`, which ships
behavioral templates for 52 LLMs). Whatever quantity that model naturally exposes
for comparing two models is acceptable here; state precisely what was used.

**M2 — Distribution** of pairwise separability: histogram + the 5th/10th/25th/50th
percentiles.

**M3 — Structural annotation of the bottom decile.** For each hard pair, label:
same base model (one is a fine-tune of the other) · adjacent versions in one
family · same organisation · same architecture family · same tokenizer · unrelated.
Report an enrichment statistic vs the base rate over all pairs, with a confidence
interval.

**M4 — The motivating case.** Does Meta-Llama-3-70B-Instruct ↔
Smaug-Llama-3-70B-Instruct land in the hard tail? Report its percentile rank.

**M5 — The ≤14B subset.** Restrict to models ≤14B and repeat M2–M3.
**This is the measurement that actually feeds D002.** Compute limits may force us
to exclude 70B-class models from the universe; if hard pairs exist *only* among
large models, invariant I1 becomes hard to satisfy and the universe decision
changes shape. Report the best ≤14B candidate confusion clusters explicitly.

**Out of scope:** do not build a corpus, do not train anything, do not implement
the separability tensor or GreedyCover. This D answers a premise question only.

### P1

**Status: PROPOSED — awaiting review.** Full plan: [`plans/D001-P1.md`](plans/D001-P1.md)
(kept as a separate file for reviewability; this section carries the summary.)

**Three repo questions D001 left open are now answered:**

- **A template is a single 384-dim centroid — intra-model spread is already
  discarded** (`LLMmap/templates.py:58`, `LLMmap/inference.py:188`; 52 keys × 384
  floats, unnormalized). D001 asked for this to be flagged if true. It is true, and
  it means **centroid distance is not a separability measure** — M1 must be measured
  from traces, not centroid geometry.
- **The only comparison quantity natively exposed is trace→centroid Euclidean
  distance**, uncalibrated (the siamese distance→probability head is absent from the
  checkpoint).
- **The shipped model is usable as-is, no trace generation needed.** 3536 test
  entries, perfectly balanced at 68 per model × 52 models; test `llm` values,
  `templates.json` keys, and `conf['llms_map']` keys are three identical sets.

**Metric.** For pair (A,B), score traces by `Δ = d(trace,T_B) − d(trace,T_A)` and
take the **AUC** separating A-traces from B-traces — symmetric, threshold-free
(required, since distances are uncalibrated), bounded, standard nonparametric CI.
Centroid distance is reported alongside so the first bullet is *evidenced*, not
merely asserted. All 1326 pairs derive from one `(3536 × 52)` distance matrix, so
the expensive embedding pass runs once.

**Steps.** S0 prereqs + full-test-set `test_model.py` baseline · S1 feature
extraction (reusing `load_datasets` + `infer_features`; cached to `.npy`) · S2
template provenance check · S3 M1/M2 · S4 M3 annotation + model-level bootstrap +
permutation test · S5 M4 · S6 M5 ≤14B subset · S7 write R and A1.

**Cost.** One `gh-dev` Slurm job, `-t 02:00:00`; **≈25–40 min wall-clock, ~0
GPU-hours** (see platform note). S2–S7 re-run off cache in seconds.

**Platform flag D001 asked for early.** aarch64/GH200 is fine — every dependency has
an ARM build. Two aarch64 blockers were found and are already fixed on `main`
(`torch.load()` ignored its `device` arg and crashed on the MPS-saved checkpoint;
`accelerate` missing from `requirements.txt`). Installed torch is `2.7.1+cpu` and
Slurm advertises no GRES on `gh`/`gh-dev`, so this runs on CPU. **Not a D002-level
finding.** Only new dependency: `matplotlib`.

**Pre-registered risk.** Only ~8–10 documented base↔fine-tune pairs exist among
these 52 models, so expected count in a 10% decile under the null is ~1 — the decile
test has power for large effects only. Mitigated by also reporting a broader
"structurally related" union label and a Mann-Whitney test over the full
related-vs-unrelated distributions. If CIs still span "no effect", the plan reports
**INCONCLUSIVE** rather than forcing a verdict.

**Blocking-ish open item.** `METHOD.md`, the TODO list, and invariants **I1–I7 are
not in this repo** — only `D001.md` was pushed. M5 exists specifically to test
whether I1 is satisfiable, and P must recommend A1, but neither I1's text nor the
A-series format is available. P1 therefore executes M5 in full and reports the ≤14B
findings **descriptively**, leaving the I1 judgment to the design side. Push those
docs and S6/S7 can tie the result to I1 directly.

## R — Results summary

> *Written by TACC-side agent. Concise. Full data lives in `results/D001/`.*

```

               statistic is computed on a variable that is censored at its ceiling
               for 88.2% of pairs, so it characterises the instrument, not the population
impact_level:  METHOD  (may escalate to INVARIANT -- design-side call, see R6)
```

**The two premises separate cleanly, and they disagree.**

| Premise | Result |
|---|---|
| **2 — hardness is structured** | **CONFIRMED, decisively.** 11 of the 12 hardest pairs are near-relatives. |
| **1 — a heavy hard tail exists** | **NOT SUPPORTED.** M6 = 0.9966; 88.2% of pairs sit exactly at AUC 1.0. |

### R1 — Headline numbers (all 52 models, 1326 pairs, 3536 test traces)

| | |
|---|---|
| mean separability | 0.99962 |
| median / p25 | 1.0 / 1.0 |
| p10 / p05 | 0.99978 / 0.99897 |
| min | 0.93880 |
| CVaR₀.₁ | 0.99622 |
| **M6 = CVaR₀.₁ / mean (A2)** | **0.9966**, model-level bootstrap CI95 **[0.9942, 0.9983]** |
| pairs at AUC exactly 1.0 | **1169 / 1326 (88.2%)** |
| pairs with AUC < 0.99 | **12** |

**Primary test (A1), structure:** related pairs are lower-separability than unrelated
across the full distribution — Mann-Whitney **p = 3.5e-32**, dominance **0.689**,
model-level bootstrap **CI95 [0.606, 0.789]** (excludes 0.5). Mean separability
0.99714 (related) vs 0.99985 (unrelated).

**Secondary (descriptive, no longer carries the verdict):** near-relative enrichment
in the bottom decile **4.07×** (34.1% observed vs 8.4% base rate), permutation
**p = 0.0000** (10k draws).

So: the *ordering* is emphatically real and structured; the *spread* is not.
CVaR₀.₁ ≈ mean because 88% of the mass is pinned at the ceiling.

### R2 — Why the falsification is instrument-attributable, not population-level

Three independent reasons the saturation is a property of this measurement:

1. **The measurement is 2-way; the task is 52-way.** Our own full-test-set
   reproduction baseline is Top-1 ≈ 88–91% over 52 classes, so the real problem has
   large headroom. Telling A from B pairwise is far easier than picking A out of 52.
   **CVaR over *pairwise* separability is measuring the wrong object.**
2. **Templates are collapsed centroids — this violates I3.** Per P1 §F1
   (`templates.py:58`, `inference.py:188`), each template is a single mean vector;
   intra-model spread is discarded. D001 therefore measured trace-cloud vs a
   *noise-free point*, which inflates separability. I3 requires point-cloud vs
   point-cloud. The shipped artifacts cannot supply that.
3. **Resolution floor.** With 68×68 = 4624 comparisons per pair, the smallest
   resolvable sub-unity AUC is 0.99978. "AUC = 1.0" means *zero errors observed*,
   not *provably separable*. 88.2% of pairs sit on this floor, so the variable CVaR
   is computed over is censored for most of its mass.

Supporting evidence for P1 §F1, now measured rather than asserted: Pearson
correlation between **centroid distance and actual separability is only r = 0.242**.
Centroid geometry is a poor proxy for separability, exactly as P1 argued.

### R3 — M4, the motivating case (Review A3 limit applies)

`Meta-Llama-3-70B-Instruct` ↔ `abacusai/Smaug-Llama-3-70B-Instruct`:
AUC **0.9888**, confusion rate 0.044, **percentile rank 0.75** — the 11th hardest of
1326 pairs. **D001's pre-registered expectation is confirmed**: the paper's worst
case is an instance of a general pattern, not an isolated one.

**Per Review A3 this is NOT a reproduction of the paper's 84%.** That number comes
from the closed-set softmax classifier (Fig. G.1); this is open-set
nearest-template. Different systems, different quantities.

### R4 — M5 / I1 and the A1 recommendation

**I1 is satisfied comfortably** in a ≤14B universe: **65 near-relative pairs among
37 models** (requirement: ≥3). Subset statistics track the full set closely
(M6 = 0.9963, dominance 0.680, p = 5.2e-17). Strict-<14B sensitivity (35 models,
Phi-3-medium excluded) is materially identical: M6 = 0.9962. The Review's call to
**include** Phi-3-medium is vindicated — it contributes a genuine hard pair
(4k↔128k, AUC 0.9853, 0.60th percentile).

Best ≤14B confusion clusters, hardest first:

|---|---|
| 0.9388 | Falcon3-7B ↔ Falcon3-10B *(hardest pair in the entire matrix)* |
| 0.9689 | Llama-3.2-1B ↔ Llama-3.2-3B |
| 0.9842 | Qwen2-1.5B ↔ Qwen2.5-0.5B |
| 0.9853 | Phi-3-medium-4k ↔ Phi-3-medium-128k |
| 0.9875 | Phi-3-mini-4k ↔ Phi-3-mini-128k |
| 0.9931 | Llama3-ChatQA-1.5-8B ↔ openchat-3.6-8b *(both Llama-3-8B derivatives)* |
| 0.9950 | Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 ↔ v0.3 |

**A1 recommendation, with one tension worth the human's attention.** The ≤14B

that is affordable is exactly D002's question.

### R5 — Interpretation limits carried into the record

- Separability = AUC of Δ = d(trace,T_B) − d(trace,T_A) under the **open-set
  nearest-template** classifier (Review A3).
- Hardness is measured **under the paper's existing 8 queries**, not hardness in
  principle — that is T2.4 and needs the full pool.
- This is **LLMmap0.2**, which the README states is not a one-to-one conversion of
  the paper's implementation. These are *our* baseline numbers; not reconciled
  against published ones.
- The AUC metric here is **scoped to D001 and is not a decision on A3**, per the
  Review's closing note.
- **S2, template provenance — resolved.** Templates recomputed from the train split
  match the shipped `templates.json` to max abs deviation **6.58e-05** against a
  template L2 scale of ~58.9 (relative ~1e-6, float32 accumulation noise). The
  shipped templates were built on **train only**, so M1 is **not** optimistically
  biased — closing the Review's conditional concern.
- Evaluation used the test split only (3536 traces, 68/model, balanced).

### R6 — What this does and does not license (design-side decision)

It does **not** license "the CDQD premise is dead". The structural premise is
confirmed at p = 3.5e-32 and the motivating case is at the 0.75th percentile. What
failed is the *exploitable-tail* premise **as measured through a saturated,
I3-violating, 2-way instrument**.

Recommended before any METHOD revision: re-measure the tail on a small pilot with a
statistic that (a) is I3-compliant (point-cloud vs point-cloud, not vs a collapsed
centroid) and (b) does not saturate — e.g. the A3 default (CV AUC of a linear probe
on point-cloud embeddings), or a k-way/confusion-based quantity that reflects the
52-way task. If the ratio stays ≈1 under a non-saturating I3-compliant statistic,
*that* would be a genuine falsification and would escalate to INVARIANT.

Flagging per the standing rule rather than working around it: **D001 as specified
could not test premise 1 on a non-saturating statistic**, because the shipped
artifacts only expose collapsed centroids.
