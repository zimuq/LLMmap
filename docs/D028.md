## D — The question

> **Can corpus v2 be built: all 85 models of `universe_v2.json` × the 259
> pool queries × 125 configs, a 16-probe multilingual extension for all 85,
> I5 embeddings, and a frozen v2 separability tensor? And can the v2 hard
> sets be frozen before any v2 pool data exists?**

### What gets generated, and what is reused

| block | models | queries × configs | source | environment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **new models** | the 48 D027 candidates | 259 × 125 (D027's frozen per-model draws: build / val / test) | **generated** | `llmmap-gpu-v2` + `d027_llm.py` (template kwargs, approved shims) |
| **drifted v1** | gemma-7b-it, gemma-2b-it, gemma-2-9b-it (D027 f1: 0.17 / 0.65 / 0.86) | 259 × 125 (replay of their own `corpus_v1` configs) | **regenerated** | `llmmap-gpu-v2` |
| **stable v1** | the other 34 v1 models (D027 f1 ≥ 0.998, plus DeciLM in its old env and internlm in its own env) | 259 × 125 | **reused from `corpus_v1` unchanged** | as generated in D006 |
| **multilingual extension** | all 85 | 16 probes (below) × 125 (each model's own configs) | **generated**, separate extension | each model's D027 generation environment |

Notes on the table:
- **Why reuse.** Under the new environment, D027's exact-batch replay
  reproduced these 34 models byte for byte. Regenerating them would only add
  sampling noise and cost.
- **internlm** drifts even within its own unchanged environment (0.50). That
  is run-to-run nondeterminism, which regeneration cannot remove, so its v1
  shard is kept.
- **The gemma three are regenerated.** Their drift is an environment effect
  (transformers 5 activation handling). In v2 they must be generated like
  every other v2-generated model. Their `corpus_v1` rows stay in v1 for v1
  analyses.
- **Generation procedure for every generated pool shard = D006's.** Per
  config, the 259 queries in chunks of the corpus batch (64), with the
  200-token ceiling. There is no cross-config packing: D027's packing was
  for the screen only. With this procedure, new and reused rows differ only
  by environment, and D027 measured that environment as neutral for the
  reused models.

### The v2 hard sets — decided 2026-10-03, frozen in S0 (before any v2 pool data)

**Human decision** (revision of `DECISIONS.md` D7 for the v2 universe):
"hard = structurally-related hard + unrelated hard". Improving either is
of value.
- **Rule (unchanged except for scope).** A pair is hard if its `paper8`
  B0′ two-logit accuracy on **S_val** is below 0.90. That is D027's 85-way,
  5-seed run, with nothing new trained.
  - D7 applied this only to near-relative pairs (N); v2 applies it to **all
    3,570 pairs**.
  - D7's restriction rested on D016: in v1, no unrelated pair fell below
    0.916. D027 shows that does not hold for recent models.
- **Strata:**
  - **H_N** = D027's H′ (38 structural pairs), unchanged; with M_N and F_N
    (= D027's M_H′, F_H′, one-step N closure).
  - **H_X** = the 19 non-N′ pairs below 0.90 from D027's population check;
    with M_X (17 models).
  - **No family closure for H_X.** It would cover 70 of the 85 models and
    turn the endpoint into the overall mean.
  - **H_all** = H_N ∪ H_X (57 pairs).
- **Stability bands for H_X** (S0): the same S_val config bootstrap as D025
  / D027 (2,000 draws, seeds averaged within a draw), from D027's saved
  per-trace S_val logits.
  - Membership stays by point estimate (as D7). The bands are reported and
    used for one sensitivity row in D029.
  - **Why this matters:** H_X was selected from ~3,400 unrelated pairs, and
    10 of the 19 lie in [0.86, 0.90]. Some may be S_val noise. D029 reads
    only S_test, which is independent of S_val, so the selection does not
    bias the comparison between methods.
- **Fresh-test note.** S_test of every new model has never been generated or
  read (D027 froze those configs unused). H_X and H_N are mostly made of new
  models, so D029's hard-set numbers will largely rest on untouched test
  data. v1 models' S_test has been read by D009–D025.

### Multilingual probe extension — fixed here (human decision "A")

**Why.**
- The 259-query pool and `paper8` are **entirely English**. Design-side
  checked: 0 non-English queries and 0 language or translation tasks.
- The tiny-aya variants (one base, regional post-training) differ by design
  in non-English coverage:
  - earth: Africa / West Asia;
  - fire: South Asia;
  - water: Asia-Pacific / West Asia / Europe;
  - global: broad.
- They are the hardest pairs in v2 (0.52–0.75 two-way).
- I5 (`multilingual-e5-large-instruct`) embeds these languages natively.

**Probes** (ids continue after D026's 259–280). Two fixed tasks, each in 8
languages chosen to cover the regional emphases:
- T1: "Please introduce yourself in two sentences."

### Pre-registered predictions for D029 — recorded now, before any v2 pool data

D029 will turn these into numeric rules before it reads any result. They
are written here, with today's date, so that the ordering is on record.
- **(P1) tiny-aya is ammunition-limited.** On the 6 tiny-aya pairs, the v2
  pool chain does **not** lift two-way accuracy to ≥ 0.90. Adding the
  multilingual probes lifts their mean by ≥ +0.05.
- **(P2) Ministral-3 is not ammunition-limited.** Its 3 size-sibling pairs
  reach ≥ 0.90 under a pool chain at some `k` ≤ 16. The differences are in
  capability, which some pool queries probe. This is the analogue of v1's
  Falcon3, which reached ≥ 0.95 at `k`=8 under linear in D025.
- **Design-side lean:** P1 likely, P2 coin-flip (the lean toward P2 is
  weaker).

### Standing rule from the human (2026-10-03), applies to D029 and later

**If a method raises the overall mean or the hard-set numbers while the
all-model guardrail fails** (all-85 mean top-1 non-inferiority at
δ = 0.02), that failure is reported as a **headline**: in R's first lines
and in the Review's "For you". It is never placed in a secondary table.
