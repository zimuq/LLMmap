## D — The question

> **Does CVaR-weighting (`γ<1`) produce a measurably better `GreedyCover`
> selection than mean-weighting (`γ=1`), once evaluated through D009's
> exact trained-classifier protocol — and does it show the same
> decisive, every-`k`-every-metric pattern D012 found for `JointGreedy`,
> or the narrower, worst-class-at-small-`k` pattern D008's own
> (untrained) proxy sweep suggested?**

D009 already trained exactly two points on this grid — `γ=1.0`
(`mean_greedy_max`) and `γ=0.1` (`cvar_max`) — as part of comparing
against `paper8`/`random`, not as a γ-ablation in its own right. D008's
full proxy sweep (`γ∈{1.0,0.5,0.25,0.1,0.05}`) was never trained beyond
those two points. This D trains the missing three.

### A structural prediction, pre-registered before running anything

**Hypothesis:** `GreedyCover`'s coverage is `MAX`-aggregated (I4), which
is monotone non-decreasing in `k` by construction (`METHOD.md §5.3`) —
*regardless of `γ`*. This is structurally different from `JointGreedy`'s
concatenated statistic, whose `γ=1.0` objective D012 found falls
monotonically past `k=1`. **Prediction: `GreedyCover`'s `γ=1.0`
selection objective will not show D012's pathology** (it should not
anti-correlate with trained accuracy) — so if `γ<1` helps `GreedyCover`
at all, it should show up as D008's own original proxy sweep suggested
(worst-class/worst-tail, concentrated at small `k`), **not** as the
uniform, every-`k`, every-metric dominance D012 found for `JointGreedy`.
**If the trained data instead shows `GreedyCover` behaving just as
decisively `γ`-dependent as `JointGreedy` did, this hypothesis is wrong
and `R` must say so plainly.**

**Update, 2026-09-18 — split verdict, from `## P` Part 0 (free, using
data that predates this D).** The *mechanism* half is confirmed:
`GreedyCover`'s objective is monotone in `k` at every γ, exactly as I4
predicts, and does not fall the way `JointGreedy`'s does — D012/R2's
pathology is `JointGreedy`-specific. **But the *prediction* half is
wrong**: D009's already-trained `γ=1.0`/`γ=0.1` endpoints show γ<1
beating γ=1 on mean top-1 at all 8 `k`, every CI excluding zero — the
D012-shaped pattern, not the narrow D008-shaped one predicted, and at a
comparable magnitude to `JointGreedy`. **This means D012/R2's own
explanation ("CVaR repairs an objective that fails outright") cannot be
the general reason γ matters** — `GreedyCover`'s objective has nothing
to repair and γ still helps just as much. Either a second, shared
mechanism exists, or D012/R2 over-attributed. Unresolved — S4's fuller
grid and S6's comparison are what this D still needs to run, but the
headline direction is already known from data that predates this D's
own drafting.

### Why this exists now

Cheap (D012 cost 9.4 minutes for a structurally identical grid), closes
`C1` symmetrically for both algorithms, and directly tests whether
D012/R2's mechanism (mean-aggregation degenerate past `k=1`) is a
`JointGreedy`-specific property of the concatenated statistic or a
general property of mean-aggregated selection. `LLMmap/greedy_cover.py`
and the trained-evaluation protocol both already exist — no new
infrastructure.

### What's already decided and does not block this D

- **`γ=1.0` and `γ=0.1` are already trained — D009's own
  `mean_greedy_max`/`cvar_max` results.** Reuse
  `results/D009/metrics_by_condition_k.json` verbatim (I6). This D
  produces the other three grid points only.
- **Grid: `γ∈{0.5, 0.25, 0.05}`**, matching D008's original proxy
  sweep's full set (not `C1`'s narrower ablate list) — `0.5` is kept
  in specifically to resolve D008/R6's own flagged anomaly ("γ=0.5 is
  non-monotone on mean top-1") with real training data, not just the
  proxy.
- **Training protocol fixed, reuse D009/D012's exactly** — same
  `hparams` hash, same split (I2), same 5-seed scheme. Varies only
  which `γ` built each `GreedyCover` `k=1..8` chain.
- **Does not touch `JointGreedy` or D012's own results** — this D is
  `GreedyCover`-only, symmetric to D012, not a re-litigation of it.

## What counts as an answer

**Same TOST-based methodology as D012 (`## P` F4 there), reused
directly rather than re-derived:** `δ=0.02` on mean top-1.

| Outcome | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **CONFIRMED, D012-shaped** | `γ<1` beats `γ=1` decisively — CI excludes 0 and `\|Δ\|` exceeds seed range — on **mean top-1, at most or all `k`**, mirroring D012's pattern for `JointGreedy`. The pre-registered structural hypothesis is wrong; mean-aggregation's degeneracy past `k=1` is not specific to the joint statistic. |
| **CONFIRMED, D008-shaped** | `γ<1` beats `γ=1` on **worst-class or worst-3 specifically, concentrated at small `k` (1–3)**, with mean top-1 showing NULL-equivalence (TOST) or no consistent effect. Matches the structural hypothesis: `GreedyCover`'s monotone objective doesn't degenerate, so `γ`'s benefit is the narrower, tail-focused one D008's proxy always suggested. |
| **NULL** | The 95% CI of every `γ`-to-`γ=1` difference lies entirely within `±δ` on every metric, every `k` — CVaR-weighting doesn't measurably help `GreedyCover` once trained, contradicting even D008's own proxy-level suggestion. |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | Neither a CONFIRMED nor NULL condition is met for a majority of `γ`/`k`/metric cells — data cannot distinguish the two CONFIRMED shapes or a real null. Report per-cell, not forced into one bucket. |
