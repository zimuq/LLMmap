## Call 1 — the label table cannot resolve under the primary readout (blocking; may need the human)

**The facts**, all from existing results; no D025 arm is trained. GLOBAL `k`=8
two-logit accuracy on S_test:

| H pair | linear concat (D017, **primary**) | headroom `1 − acc` | attention (D016, 5-seed) | headroom |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Falcon3 | 0.96 | **0.04** | 0.832 | 0.168 |
| Phi-3-medium | 0.82 | 0.18 | 0.732 | 0.268 |
| Mistral v0.2/v0.3 | 0.96 | **0.04** | 0.896 | 0.104 |
| Llama 1B/3.1-8B | 0.98 | **0.02** | 0.968 | **0.032** |

**Consequences under the D's table (M = 0.05):**
- BUDGET-LIMITED needs `a(p)` ≥ +0.05. `a(p)` ≤ headroom, so it is
  **impossible** under linear for Falcon, Mistral and Llama, and impossible
  under attention for Llama.
- POOL-LIMITED needs hi(`b`) < +0.05. With headroom ≤ 0.04, `b` ≤ 0.04 for
  every draw, so it fires **automatically**. The label would say "the pool
  can't separate them" about pairs the readout already separates at 96–98%.
- **Pooled label under linear:** the pooled mean of `a` is bounded by the mean
  headroom (0.065). The pooled row is mostly ceiling.

**Why this happened.** The D's motivation quotes attention numbers (Phi
.67–.73, Falcon .80–.85), and H is defined by the attention protocol (B0). The
primary readout was then switched to linear, which D017 showed is stronger. On
this corpus it is strong enough to nearly solve three of the four "hard" pairs
at `k`=8. That is itself informative (R will report it), but it breaks the
labels.

**Options:**
- **(a) Ceiling rule, keep linear primary.** A pair is labelled **CEILING**
  (not POOL-LIMITED) if `1 − acc_GLOBAL(p, 8) < M` on the readout in question.
  CEILING pairs are excluded from the pooled label and reported separately.
  Under linear, that leaves Phi-3-medium alone as the pooled population.
- **(b) Ceiling rule, attention primary** *(TACC recommends)*. H was defined
  with the attention protocol, and the D's motivating numbers are attention
  numbers. Under attention, three pairs have headroom ≥ 0.10 and only Llama is
  CEILING.
  - Linear becomes the secondary row, with the same ceiling rule.
  - Cost of the switch: attention intervals are wider (hierarchical
    config×seed; Call 3).
  - The linear row also carries a finding of its own: under linear, the
    limiting factor for three pairs is the readout, not the queries.
- **(c) Relative margin.** Replace the absolute M by "closes ≥ 50% of GLOBAL's
  remaining error". This works under either readout, but it changes M's
  meaning (the D chose 5 pp deliberately, as the decision-relevant size).

**Recommendation: (b), with the ceiling rule stated exactly:**
- CEILING if `1 − acc_GLOBAL(p, 8)` < 0.05, evaluated on the full-data point
  estimate of that readout before any SPEC arm is trained.
- On the facts above: attention → Llama CEILING; linear → Falcon, Mistral and
  Llama CEILING.
- Pooled labels run over the non-CEILING pairs of each readout.

**This changes the pre-registered primary readout and adds a label**, so
design-side may need to take it to the human. **TACC does not start S1 until
this is decided.** The facts in the table were read from D016/D017 files
design-side already had; nothing D025-specific was trained or looked at.

## Call 2 — retrain GLOBAL `k`=1..8 attention instead of reusing D010's counts

**The gap.**
- D010 kept only seed-0 checkpoints at `k`=1..8. D016 has seeds 1–4, but only
  at `k`=8, and only S_test logits.
- D010's stored counts are `cnt_total`, `cnt_model`, and `cnt_pair`
  (combined two-logit, 65 N pairs).
- The D's secondaries need quantities those counts lack:
  - (a) family-restricted recall;
  - (b) directional accuracies;
  - (c) placebo on other H pairs, derivable, but directional needs logits;
  - (d) 666-pair tail.

**Proposal.**
- Retrain GLOBAL `k`=1..8 attention (40 runs, about 6 GPU-min), using D010's
  chain in D010's order.
- **Stop condition:** assert that `cnt_total`, `cnt_model` and `cnt_pair`
  reproduce D010's stored counts exactly. Seeded retrains are bit-exact
  (D016, D019, D024).
- Every arm then yields the same logit-derived counts.

## Call 3 — bootstrap details

- **Linear (config-only).** `M = boot_draws(25, 2000, seed=20260929)`, one set
  of draws shared by all arms, pairs, `k` and both splits (as the D states).
  - Pair accuracy per draw: `M·cnt / (2·ΣM)`, where `cnt` is the per-config
    two-logit correct count (0–2).
  - Pooled: the mean over the pooled pairs within each draw.
- **Attention (hierarchical).** D023's approved scheme, with seed 20260929:
  - configs: the same `M`;
  - seed indices: `S = default_rng(20260929).integers(0, 5, (2000, 16, 5))`;
  - arm X at budget `k` uses `S[:, k−1]`, shared across arms at the same `k`.
- So `a(p)` (both arms at `k`=8) resamples seeds **jointly**, the D023 Call-1
  rationale. `b(p)` (SPEC at 16 vs GLOBAL at 8) uses independent seed slots,
  because different `k` are separate trainings.
- The 4/5 seed-variance understatement carries over as a disclosure.

## Call 4 — minor, disclosed rather than changed

- **FAM's CVaR is maximin for three groups.** With m = ⌊0.1·|P|⌋ ≥ 1, it
  reduces to the minimum within-family pair for Mistral (15 pairs) and Phi-3
  (10), and to the worst 2 pairs for Llama (21). That follows the D's formula,
  and no change is proposed.
- **S_val attention checkpoints.** The attention network selects its
  checkpoint on S_val, so the S_val replication row (e) is linear only, as the
  D says. That is consistent: row (e) is not run under attention.
- **Slot order.** Each chain trains in its own order (the D's convention).
  SPEC and GLOBAL share no identical-input `k` unless their prefixes coincide.
  S1 lists any that do, and those get exact-reproduction asserts against
  GLOBAL's counts.

## Steps after approval

**S1 — Chains** (CPU `gh` job, a few minutes). **Freeze, commit and push
before any training.**
- Selection goes through one wrapper, `d025_lib.select(P, k)`, which is
  `LLMmap.joint_greedy.joint_greedy(XY2[:, P], XX2, ia[P], ib[P], k, 0.1,
  "energy")` on D010's precomputed accumulators (`d010_select.precompute`).
  Only P changes.
- **Code-path check:** `select(all 666, 8)` must equal
  `[193, 140, 237, 114, 233, 117, 0, 16]` (stop condition).
- The arms:
  - GLOBAL to `k`=16, with the prefix asserted;
  - SPEC(p) for the 4 H pairs;
  - FAM(Mistral), FAM(Llama), FAM(Phi-3); FAM(Falcon) = SPEC(Falcon),
    asserted.
- Also recorded:
  - objective traces and peak `k`;
  - overlaps with GLOBAL;
  - D022 union flags;
  - identical-input `k` lists;
  - the Call 1 CEILING table (pre-training, from GLOBAL's existing numbers).
- → `results/D025/selection.json`.

**S2 — Training.** Sharded one job per chain on `gh`, so jobs run in parallel
under `MaxJobsPU` = 20.
- **Attention.** D009 protocol (`d009_train.run_one`, `hparams_from_shipped`,
  hparams hash asserted equal to D009's, S_val early stopping, S_test once),
  seeds 0–4, `k`=1..16.
  - GLOBAL covers `k`=1..16, with the `k`≤8 reproduction asserts of Call 2.
  - **All checkpoints are saved**, about 640 × 34 MB ≈ 22 GB on `$WORK`
    (183 GB used of 1 TB).
  - Each checkpoint is reloaded and `mean_top1` asserted.
- **Linear.** D017 concat (C=1.0, lbfgs, `tol`=1e-6, `max_iter`=20000), fit on
  S_build, logits on S_test **and** S_val.
  - GLOBAL `k`≤8 must reproduce D017's per-pair values and D020's `JointGreedy`
    concat counts exactly (stop condition).
- **Counts per (arm, readout, `k`, seed):**
  - `dir666` (666 × 2 × 25): directional two-logit correct per config;
  - `fam_{group}` (|F| × 25): family-restricted argmax correct;
  - `cnt_total` and `cnt_model` (37-way). These are **not** reported as a
    result for SPEC/FAM chains, per the D.
- → `run_counts.npz`, `linear_counts.npz`, `runs.json`.

**S3 — Analysis** (CPU).
- **Primary labels** per pair and pooled, under the Call 1 decision (CEILING
  rule), for both readouts. If the two readouts disagree on a label, both are
  reported and the disagreement is flagged.
- **Secondaries:**
  - (a) family-restricted recall, FAM vs GLOBAL, `k`=1..16, with `a_F`, `b_F`
    and labels (descriptive);
  - (b) directional accuracies for every arm;
  - (c) cross-specialist placebo at `k`=8 and 16;
  - (d) GLOBAL `k`=9..16: mean top-1, F_H recall and M_H recall, plus the
    mandatory 666-pair tail plot at `k`=8 vs 16;
  - (e) S_val row, linear only;
  - (f) stably-hard restriction (Falcon, Phi-3-medium);
  - (g) by-products: identical-answer counts (above), objective traces,
    overlaps and flags.
- → `analysis.json`, `curves.png`, `tail_k8_k16.png`.
