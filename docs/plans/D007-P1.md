## F1 — BLOCKING(ish): D007 does not specify the **analysis-time token budget**, and the corpus makes it a live choice

D006 generated at C7's **200-token ceiling** and, because greedy/ancestral
generation is causal (D006 A2, verified 100/100), any budget ≤200 is recoverable
by truncation. That was the whole point of the ceiling: **the generation budget
and the analysis budget are separate knobs.**

D007 inherits the second knob without naming it. The embeddings currently in
`data/corpus_v1/embeddings/` were computed on the **full 200-token** responses,
so building the tensor today silently sets the analysis budget to 200 — and I6
then freezes that.

**This matters because D006/R2's C7 pilot found the opposite of what one would
assume**: separability was significantly *best* at 100 tokens (probe AUC 0.8205 @
100 vs 0.8083 @ 200, better on 3/15 pairs going up, p=0.001 paired). **That result
is currently marked PROVISIONAL** (D006/R10) because it was computed on
*normalised* embeddings, and normalisation was subsequently found to discard
real signal (+0.051 AUC raw, better on 15/15 pairs).

So the honest position is: **we do not currently know the best analysis budget,
and D007 would freeze one by default.**

**Proposal — settle it inside D007, cheaply, on far better evidence than the
pilot had.** Re-embed the corpus a second time at a 100-token truncation
(~12 minutes, text is stored; ~2.45 GB more on `$WORK`) and build `S_probe` at
both budgets on the **full 666 pairs** rather than the pilot's 15. Then freeze the
budget that wins, with the comparison on record. This also discharges R10's
provisional flag as a by-product rather than leaving it hanging over A3 and query
design.

**Cost of doing it:** one extra embedding pass (~12 min) + one extra `S_probe`
build (1.08 core-hours). **Cost of not doing it:** a frozen tensor at a budget
chosen by default, which I6 makes expensive to revisit.

I recommend doing it. But *which* budget to freeze is a Part-C-shaped call, so
the comparison is mine to run and the choice is design side's to make.

## F2 — BLOCKING: I4's MAX-over-queries aggregation is **upward-biased**, and D007's falsification criterion is a threshold on exactly that quantity

This is a statistical problem with the criterion itself, and I would rather raise
it now than produce a verdict that inherits it.

D007's S5 computes per-pair coverage as `max_q S_probe[q][p]` (I4: MAX, never
sum/mean) and applies `TODO.md` T1.6's rule: **10th percentile > 0.9 ⇒ TAIL STILL
ABSENT**, which is a STOP escalating to a `METHOD.md`/I1 revision.

Each cell's AUC is estimated from **75 vs 75 points in 1024 dimensions**. That is
`p ≫ n`, and the estimate is correspondingly noisy — an order of magnitude fewer
points per cloud than the S6b pilot's 600 vs 600. **Taking the MAX over 259 noisy
estimates is a winner's curse: `E[max]` exceeds the true max whenever the
estimates carry variance**, and the bias grows with both the noise and the number
of queries maximised over.

The direction is the dangerous one. Upward bias on `max_q` pushes the per-pair
coverage distribution **up**, which pushes the 10th percentile **up**, which is
precisely the direction that triggers **TAIL STILL ABSENT** — a STOP verdict, and
a `METHOD.md` revision escalation, potentially caused by estimator noise rather
than by the models being genuinely easy to tell apart.

This is the same shape as D001's censoring failure — a property of the
*instrument* masquerading as a property of the population — which is why D004's
resolution audit became mandatory. **D007's S4 audit as written checks for
saturation (cells at ceiling); it does not check for selection bias in the
aggregation step**, so it would not catch this.

**Proposal — measure the bias, do not assume it away.** A standard
winner's-curse correction, cheap here:

1. Split the 75 build configs into two disjoint halves, A and B (~37/38).
2. On half A, compute per-query AUC and take `argmax_q` per pair — the *selected*
   query.
3. Evaluate **that same query** on half B. This is an honest, selection-free
   estimate of the selected query's separability.
4. `max_on_A − value_on_B`, aggregated over the 666 pairs, **is** the bias.

Report the corrected distribution alongside the naive one, and **apply T1.6's
10th-percentile criterion to the honest estimate.** If the two agree, the verdict
is robust and the extra work cost ~1 core-hour. If they disagree, the naive
criterion was about to produce a STOP for the wrong reason.

Adds ~1.1 core-hours (one extra `S_probe`-shaped pass over half-splits). Cheap
against the cost of a wrong falsification.

**I am not proposing to change T1.6's threshold** — that is design side's and the
human's. I am proposing that the quantity it is applied to be an unbiased one.

## F3 — `S_energy` needs a scale-free variant, and the raw one is not comparable across queries

D004 reported energy distance raw and scale-free, and D007's S3 asks for both.
Worth stating why it binds harder here: with the **unnormalised** embeddings
(D006/R10) the mean vector norm is ≈19.6, and response-length differences across
queries will move that norm. Raw energy distance is therefore **not comparable
across queries**, and I4's MAX runs *across queries*. The scale-free variant
(energy distance divided by the pooled within-cloud mean distance) is the one that
may be aggregated; the raw one is diagnostic only. I will report both and use the
scale-free one for any MAX.

## Steps

**S1 — Confirm `READY`.** ✅ Done: manifest `cdqd-corpus-v1`, `status: READY`,
37/37, invariants re-verified. Re-asserted in code at run time, not assumed.

**S2 — Build `S_probe` (259 × 666).** Per (query, pair): model *i*'s 75
build-split embeddings for that query vs model *j*'s (I3: point cloud vs point
cloud), 5-fold CV linear probe, fixed `C = 1.0` (not tuned per cell — D004's
`p ≫ n` leakage reasoning applies identically). Parallelised over cells with
`joblib`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` per worker to avoid oversubscription. Store as
`float32` with a per-cell sample-count array (F4).

**S2a — Pilot ~500 cells first**, spanning easy and near-relative pairs, and check
the AUC distribution before committing the full run. Specifically: what fraction
is at ceiling at `n=75`? The S6b pilot saw 0% at ceiling with 600 points per
cloud; 75 is a different regime, and if the bounded statistic saturates here then
A3's audit-as-gate fires and `S_energy` becomes authoritative. **Cheap, and it is
the difference between discovering that after 1 core-hour or after the fact.**

**S3 — Build `S_energy` (259 × 666)**, raw + scale-free (F3), same pairing.

**S4 — Resolution audit as a gate.** For both tensors: fraction of cells at/near
ceiling and floor; fraction of **pairs** at ceiling after I4's MAX; number of
distinct values. Plus, per F2, the aggregation-bias estimate. Per A3: if
`S_probe` saturates, `S_energy` is authoritative for interpretation.

**S5 — T1.6 hard-tail check.** `max_q` per pair (I4: MAX), full distribution and
percentiles, under both statistics, **and under both the naive and the
bias-corrected estimator (F2)**. Apply T1.6's 10th-percentile criterion to the
corrected estimate; report the naive one alongside.

**S6 — Motivating case.** `Falcon3-10B ↔ Falcon3-7B` percentile rank under both
statistics, plus pairs that rank materially differently from D004 (different
representation, different query pool — a change is expected and informative).
**Also report `Llama3-ChatQA-1.5-8B`**: D006/R11 pre-registered the prediction
that its canned-refusal mode (355/500 unique rows vs a median of 497; *" Sorry. I
cannot find the answer based on the context."* ×86) should make it unusually easy
to separate. If it is not, the instrument is suspect — a free sanity check on the
whole tensor.

**S7 — Write `## R`.** Verdict, full distributions (not just the ratio), S4's
audit under every verdict branch, the representation caveat stated regardless of
outcome, tensor shape and schema tag (I7, distinct from D006's corpus tag).

## Failure handling

- **S2a shows `S_probe` saturating at n=75.** Do not proceed as if it does not.
  Report, and let `S_energy` carry the interpretation per A3. This is the branch
  A3's gate was written for.
- **Bias correction (F2) flips the verdict.** Report both, recommend the
  corrected one, escalate — do not pick silently.
- **Wall-clock ≫ measured.** Guardrail: stop and report past 6 core-hours
  (~4.5× the measurement).
- **Any pair with < 75 points per cloud.** Should be impossible (all shards
  validated); assert rather than proceed.

## Verification

- Tensor shape exactly `(259, 666)`; pair index reproducible from a sorted model
  list, stored explicitly rather than implied by iteration order.
- Self-consistency: a model against itself would give AUC ≈ 0.5 — spot-check a
  few synthetic self-pairs as an instrument check.
- `S_probe` and `S_energy` should agree in *direction* on pair ordering (Spearman
  ρ > 0), as they did in D004. Disagreement is a finding, not a bug to hide.
- Re-running from stored embeddings must reproduce the tensor bit-for-bit
  (seeded CV).
