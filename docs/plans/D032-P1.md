**S0.2 Scores.**

**S0.3 Similarities.**

  "centred cos"). float64.
- Record each similarity's distribution over query pairs (min, p05, median, p95, max), and the rank
  correlation between PP-pearson and TXT-ccos over query pairs (descriptive, for secondary (d)).

- Reused verbatim, no training: TOP-K[CVaR], MMR-PP[CVaR] × 6 (D031 chains asserted equal in S0; D031
  counts both readouts, `k` = 1–8, all 6 variants, attention 5 seeds), JG, PAPER8, GC, RANDOM.

- Consequence: MMR-PP[CVaR]'s non-starred variants have attention at `k` = 1–8 (D031 Call 6), the new
  non-starred ones only at `k` = 8 (the D's grid). The WORSE scan uses `k` = 8 only, so the asymmetry does
  not touch any label.

### Call 2 — v1 thread count for S1 (needs a decision)
- The D fixes S2's threads (v2 8, v1 16) but not S1's. D031's S1 ran at 8 threads in both views; its v1
  S2 ran at 16.
- **Recommended (a):** S1 at S2's threads (v2 8, v1 16), so every new star is chosen under the conditions
  it is then trained in. MMR-PP[CVaR]\* stays D031's choice (the D), and as a **diagnostic only** S1 also
  re-scores D031's 6 v1 MMR-PP[CVaR] variants at 16 threads (6 extra fits, S_val only) and reports whether
  `MMR|pearson|0.7` is still the S_val maximum. The star is not changed either way.
- (b) S1 at 8 threads in both views (D031's S1 conditions), at the cost of v1 new stars being chosen under
  different fits than they are trained with.

### Call 3 — TXT similarity variants run as written (disclosure)
- e5 embeddings are anisotropic: uncentred cosine between unrelated texts is typically high and
  compressed. MMR-TXT|cos may therefore barely diversify, the failure mode D031 A1 fixed for PP. The D
  already includes the centred variant (ccos) and the S_val choice picks between them, so no change is
  proposed; S0 reports both distributions so the Review can see whether the compression occurred.
- The redundancy term uses `max_{s ∈ S} sim(q, s)` as in D031 (not mean), for both spaces.

### Call 4 — TOP-K[MEAN] and HELM-RANK (disclosure)
- D031 found r̂ = 1 everywhere, so HELM-RANK|sf ranks by the loading on the dominant direction of the same
  scale-free tensor — close to a weighted mean over pairs (D031 Review Call 2). TOP-K[MEAN] may therefore
  overlap HELM-RANK|sf heavily (D031: HELM-RANK was the weakest arm). S0 reports the overlap; nothing
  changes in the design.

### Call 5 — identical-input asserts and the thread check (disclosure)
- Attention identical-input cells reproduce D031 / D029 / D010 counts exactly (D031 showed attention is
  bit-reproducible on this path).
- Linear: the TOP-K[CVaR] `k` = 8 refit must equal D031's stored counts in both views (v2 at 8, v1 at 16
  threads). If v2 fails at 8 threads, the job stops and R reports it (no silent thread change).

### Call 6 — near-relative lists (disclosure)
- v2 uses N′ (161 structural pairs; D027), **not** H_all (57, selected on LLMmap errors; D030 lesson B1).
  v1 uses the 65 structural pairs. Both are rule-based (same base / lineage) and fixed before any tensor.

### Call 7 — sharding and wall time (disclosure)
- gh-dev 2 h wall, 1 running job. Shards sized from D031's measured rates (v2 linear ≈ 2.2 fit-min per
  worker at 8 threads; v1 ≈ 2 fits/min at 4 × 16 threads; attention v2 ≈ 12 s/run, v1 ≈ 5 s/run).
- Each shard writes on completion; no shard is projected above 70 min.
