## Call 1 — concatenation has a small slot-order floor at the converged solver

**What the D assumes.** "No training-seed axis on either side … a paired bootstrap
CI excluding zero is sufficient on its own." True for seeds. But there is one
other non-sampling source the D doesn't mention.

**What the stored numbers show.** At `k`=2, JointGreedy is `[193, 140]` and
H1-linear is `[140, 193]`: the **same set in a different slot order**. Both were fit
at D017's frozen `max_iter=20000, tol=1e-6`:

| readout | JointGreedy `[193,140]` (D017) | H1-linear `[140,193]` (D018) | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| concat | 0.844324 | 0.845405 | **+1 trace** (1/925) |
| mean-pool | 0.831351 | 0.831351 | 0 |

**Control: it is the order, not the job.** The same set in the *same* order,
`[193,140]` (JointGreedy in D017, H1-attention in D018), reproduces
exactly across the two jobs (concat 0.844324 both, mean-pool 0.831351 both). So
does `k`=1 `{193}` (0.727568 both). Cross-job determinism holds, and the 1-trace gap
comes from slot order.

D017's Call 1 established order-invariance at this solver setting on **one**
permutation of `paper8` `k`=8 (gap 0.000000). The theory holds: the convex
optimum is order-free. But lbfgs stops at `tol=1e-6` short of the exact optimum,
and on this cell that leaves one test trace on the other side of an argmax.
Mean-pool is symmetric in the slots, so it has no such floor.

**Why it matters here and nowhere else.** Every earlier linear comparison had
large deltas. Here the motivating deltas are 9–27 traces, so 1 trace is small
but not zero. Without the seed-range guard, a CI can exclude zero on a delta of
a few traces.

**Proposal (≈1 min extra):** in S0, refit each of the four chains at `k`=8,
concat, in **reversed slot order** (4 fits). Report the order gap per chain as
the concat **numerical floor**, and flag any concat cell whose |Δ| is at or below
the largest observed gap. That flag is *not* a resolution rule (the D's criterion
stands); it's a disclosure attached to the cell. The `k`=2 JointGreedy/H1-linear
cell is reported as an **order-only cell** (same set, different order), not as a
comparison.

## Call 2 — pre-register the primary question; everything else is descriptive

The D asks for 4 deltas × 8 `k` × 2 poolings × up to 4 metrics, with "paired 95%
CI excludes zero" per cell and no seed-range guard. On `mean_top1` alone that's
64 cells (60 after removing the identical-input ones below). Under a global null
about 3 would show "resolved" by chance, and all four metrics make it ~13. The
D's criterion is right *per cell*. The risk is reading a scattering of resolved
cells as a pattern.

**Proposal — fixed now, before any number exists:**

- **Primary (the question the D exists to settle):** `JointGreedy − H1-linear`,
  `mean_top1`, `k`=4..8, **both poolings** (the cells D018/R3 flagged).
  - **RESOLVED** if the paired CI excludes zero at a `k` **and** the sign is the
    same at every `k`=4..8 in that pooling (the consistent-sign criterion D017
    and D018 already used).
  - **NOT RESOLVED** otherwise, reported per `k`.
- **Secondary (descriptive, per-cell, as the D asks):** the other three deltas,
  all `k`=1..8, and the other three metrics. Each cell carries its CI and a
  resolved flag under the D's criterion. `R` states the total cell count and the
  number resolved next to the ~5% chance expectation. No Bonferroni: this project
  hasn't used one, and adding one now would be a new framework the D rules out.

**Identical-input cells, excluded from every count** (same set, same order, so Δ
≡ 0 by construction):

- `JointGreedy − H1-attention` at `k`=1, 2
- `GreedyCover − H1-attention` at `k`=1

**Order-only cell** (Call 1): `JointGreedy − H1-linear` at `k`=2.

## Steps

**S0** — Record S0(a) as above. Refit GreedyCover (D008 `cvar_max` chain) and
JointGreedy (D010 `joint_energy` chain) under both poolings, `k`=1..8, with D017's

- `LogisticRegression(C, solver="lbfgs", max_iter=20000, tol=1e-6)`, no scaler
- C = 1.0 concat / 3.0 mean-pool
- concat features: `a.reshape(n, -1)`; mean-pool features: `a.mean(axis=1)`
- `build_traces` on `build` → `test`

**Assert** all four stored metrics reproduce D017 exactly (|diff| < 1e-9), and do
the same for H1-attention/H1-linear against D018's `H1-*|linear_*` entries. Assert
no fit hits `max_iter`. Also run Call 1's 4 reversed-order `k`=8 concat fits.

**S1** — One `boot_draws(25, 2000)` (seed 20260908, as every D since D009) shared
across all 4 chains × 2 poolings × 8 `k`. `boot_metrics(st, M)` per cell. There is
no seed averaging because the fits are deterministic.

**S2** — The four deltas (`paired_ci(a, b)`, a − b), all `k`, both poolings, all
four metrics. Primary verdict per Call 2. Identical-input and order-only cells
labelled, not counted.

**S3** — `## R`: reproduced point estimates (should equal D018/R3 and D017 exactly),
the primary verdict, the secondary table with the cell count, and the concat order
floor.

## Failure handling

- **A refit does not reproduce D017/D018 exactly:** stop and report. The
  determinism premise the whole D rests on would be broken.
- **A fit hits `max_iter`:** stop and report. Do not loosen the solver (D017/Call 1,
  D018/Call 3).
