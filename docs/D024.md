## D — The question

> **The selection algorithm is held fixed: JointGreedy, greedy
> CVaR₀.₁ over the 666 pairs of a set-level statistic, nested `k`=1..8.
> Only the fingerprint definition inside that statistic changes. Does the
> chain selected on point clouds (the current JointGreedy) train to higher
> accuracy than chains selected on (i) per-model centroids, i.e. the same
> data with the within-model spread discarded, and (ii) a single reference
> config per model?**

### Three fingerprint definitions and what each comparison isolates

| arm | a model `v`'s fingerprint for query `q` | set-level statistic between `v`, `v'` | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`CLOUD`** | the 75 S_build response embeddings (the point cloud, I3) | scale-free joint energy distance (D010) | **existing JointGreedy chain, reused verbatim** |
| **`CENTROID`** | the mean of those 75 embeddings | distance between concatenated centroids, per-query between-scale normalised (below) | new |
| **`SINGLE`** | one embedding, from one reference config chosen by a fixed rule (below) | the same distance, on single points | new |

- **CLOUD vs CENTROID** isolates the **within-model spread**. Both use all
  75 configs, and only the spread is dropped. This is the novelty claim.
- **CENTROID vs SINGLE** isolates **multi-config reference data**. It is
  the selection-level analogue of AdaptPrint's Gen → All jump.
- **Descriptive reference row:** D023's `Greedy-ADD` (the cloud with
  whitening), reused. It completes a ladder: single point → centroid →
  cloud → whitened cloud. No new training is needed for it.

### Definitions — fixed here

- **CENTROID statistic.**
  - For each query, `d²_q(v,v') = ‖μ_vq − μ_v'q‖²`, with `μ_vq` the mean
    S_build embedding.
  - **Per-query normalisation, centroid-only:** divide `d²_q` by its median
    over the 666 pairs for that query. This uses no within-model
    information. It exists only so that no single query dominates through
    response-embedding norm (the same reason D007/F3 made the energy
    statistic scale-free).
  - Set statistic: `sqrt(Σ_{q∈Q} d²_q / med_q)`.
  - **Sensitivity row:** the raw, un-normalised version.
- **SINGLE reference config.** Configs are drawn per model (TACC_NOTES
  Issue 18), so "the same config for every model" does not exist. Per
  model, in S_build, take the first match in this order:
  1. no system prompt, no RAG, no CoT;
  2. system prompt exactly "You are a helpful assistant.", no RAG, no CoT;
  3. no RAG, no CoT.

  Within the chosen tier, take the lowest temperature, then the lowest
  config index. Use that config's embeddings for all 259 queries, with the
  same normalised statistic as CENTROID (medians computed on the single
  points). S0 reports which tier each model got.
- **Selection** for CENTROID and SINGLE: greedy, maximising CVaR₀.₁ over
  the 666 pairs (`m` = 66, the `cvar()` definition), nested `k`=1..8, ties
  to the lowest query id. The same code shape as `LLMmap/joint_greedy.py`,
  with the statistic swapped. **Code-path check:** the same wrapper with the
  CLOUD statistic must reproduce D010's chain exactly.

### What is and is not known going in

- CLOUD's trained numbers exist for both readouts (D010, D020), with 666-pair
  counts at `k`=8 (D016). Nothing needs rerunning (I6).
- The chains may share early queries (193 is everyone's first pick so far).
  Shared-prefix `k` are identical inputs; handle them exactly as in D023
  (exact zeros, reproduction asserts, PARITY on differing `k`).
- **Exploratory label:** the same 25-config S_test. The three chains are
  frozen for the fresh-S_test D whatever the outcome.

## What counts as an answer

**Primary endpoint, used for each comparison.** The quantity is the mean
over `k`=1..8 of Δ mean top-1, on the attention readout, with D023's
approved hierarchical bootstrap: configs paired, seeds resampled jointly
across arms, 2,000 draws, seed 20260928, plus an independent-seed
sensitivity row. δ = 0.02.

| comparison | role |
|---|---|
| **(P1) CLOUD − CENTROID** | **the novelty claim** |
| **(P2) CENTROID − SINGLE** | the multi-config-reference claim |
| CLOUD − SINGLE | reported, no separate verdict |

| outcome (each of P1, P2) | criterion |
|---|---|
| **BETTER** | lower bound > 0 (mean over `k`=1..8) |
| **PARITY** | interval ⊂ [−δ, +δ] (mean over *differing* `k`) |
| **WORSE** | upper bound < 0 |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise |

Verdict strings implement the full rule. D018's label lesson applies.

**Secondary (descriptive, per `k` where applicable):**

- (a) **Linear readouts** (concat, mean-pool): the same quantities, config
  bootstrap only.
- (b) **Predictor footing, D021-style.** On D016's 666-pair `k`=8 target,
  over its three chains:
  - pooled Spearman of the CENTROID and SINGLE statistics next to D021's
    `MAX`/`JOINT-E`/`ADD` values;
  - LOMO jackknife, and Δρ(`JOINT-E` − CENTROID) paired.
  - This says whether dropping the spread already hurts *ranking*, before
    selection.
- (c) **Hard cases at `k`=8:**
  - CVaR₀.₁ over the 666 pairs' trained two-logit accuracy, the worst pair,
    and the two named pairs, per D023/S-b;
  - D019's four hard-model sets, restricted recall with seed ranges.
- (d) **Chains:** overlaps among the three arms and with paper8, and each
  chain's family composition (D022's labels). Also report the union-flag
  count per chain from D022's `query_flags.json`, descriptive only.

## Constraints

**I3 — deliberately violated inside two ablation arms, human-approved.**
I3 says separability is computed point-cloud vs point-cloud, never by
collapsing the cloud.
- CENTROID collapses the cloud to its mean. SINGLE uses one response per
  model per query.
- **That is the point of this D:** it tests I3's own premise at the
  selection level.
- Both statistics exist only as ablation arms. Nothing in the method, the
  frozen tensor, or any other D's statistic changes, and neither arm may be
  reused as a selection statistic elsewhere without a new human decision.

**Other invariants.**
- I2: S_build-only selection; the standard split protocol for training.
- I5/I6: frozen embeddings and corpus; CLOUD, Greedy-ADD and paper8 reused
  verbatim.
- I4: untouched (no coverage statistic is involved).
- I7: no corpus change.

**Out of scope:** GreedyCover or ILP variants of the three definitions,
other γ, MMD versions, the fresh S_test, and any reinterpretation of D021's
predictor verdict.

**Cost:**
- selection: CPU minutes;
- training: 2 arms × 8 `k` × 5 seeds ≈ 80 attention runs plus linear
  fits, ≈ 15 GPU-min by D023's measurement.
- **State the measured cost in `R`.**

  "large separation".

**Design-side error, recorded plainly.**
- The D prescribed per-query median normalisation "so that no single
  query dominates", meant as anti-strawman. It divides out *between-model*
  scale, which is the centroid's only signal. CLOUD's scale-free statistic
  divides by *within-model* spread instead.
- The result was an 8 pp handicap, first visible at `k`=1 (query 78 at
  0.41 vs 193 at 0.63).
- **Lesson for any future baseline:** pre-register the plausible variants
  of a baseline and compare against the strongest. A "fairness"
  transformation is itself a modelling choice and can cut either way. The
  amendment worked as a guard; it should be the default, not an
  afterthought.
- TACC's R1 hypothesis (within-spread vs between-scale normalisation) is
  plausible and untested. It is not needed for any claim.

**Mechanism, combining D021 and D024.**
- On *ranking* (fixed chains), the raw cloud barely beats the centroid:
  Δρ +0.038 [−0.030, +0.107]. The *whitened* cloud (`ADD`) does beat it,
  by +0.203 [+0.069, +0.337].
- On *selection*, the raw cloud's energy statistic beats the best centroid
  by +1.5 pp.
- The consistent reading: the spread matters as a **noise yardstick**
  (separation relative to within-model variation). The more explicitly
  that is used (whitening), the better the ranking. The effect on selected
  accuracy is small because the multi-config centroid already captures
