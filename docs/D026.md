## D — The question

> **Do about 20 new probes, written to target what actually differs
> between Phi-3-medium-128k-instruct and Phi-3-medium-4k-instruct, add
> material separation for this pair beyond the best that the existing
> 259-query pool achieves, under an identical selection-and-evaluation
> procedure?**

### Why these probes

- The twins share a base model, a size and a tokenizer. Their names differ
  only in supported context length (4k vs 128k). They give byte-identical
  greedy answers on 46/259 pool queries (D024 S0).
- D025's best chain on this pair (the Phi-3 family specialist, .86–.90)
  opens with **self-description** queries: 9, "What is your parameter
  count?", and 65, "Are you a generative or retrieval model?". Refusal
  probes, which the pair specialist picked, did worse than the global
  chain.
- Hence three families: two short and benign, one long and
  deployment-dependent.

### The probes — fixed here, before any generation

New query ids continue from 259, in this order.

**F1 — context-length self-knowledge (short):**

| id | text |
|---|---|
| 259 | What is the maximum context length you support, in tokens? |
| 260 | How long can my input be before you start losing track of its beginning? Give a number of tokens. |
| 261 | Is your context window 4K tokens or 128K tokens? Answer with just one of the two. |
| 262 | If I send you a 300-page book in one message, can you read all of it? Answer yes or no, then state your context limit. |
| 263 | Describe your context window in one sentence. |
| 264 | Were you trained for long-context tasks? Answer briefly. |
| 265 | What is the full name of your model, including any context-length suffix? |
| 266 | Roughly how many words can you take as input at once? |

**F2 — self-description (short; neighbours of queries 9 and 65):**

| id | text |
|---|---|
| 267 | How many parameters do you have? Answer with a single number. |
| 268 | Which company trained you, and what is your exact model version? |
| 269 | Are you a small, medium, or large language model? Explain in one sentence. |
| 270 | What architecture are you based on, and how many layers do you have? |
| 271 | What is your knowledge cutoff date? |
| 272 | List three facts about yourself: your name, your size, and your context length. |

**F3 — long-input retrieval (deployment-dependent):**
- Ids 273–278: a needle sentence, then filler of length L tokens, then the
  question.
- Needles: "The secret word is *marmalade*." and "The secret word is
  *lighthouse*."
- Question: "What was the secret word mentioned at the very beginning of
  this message? Answer with one word."
- L ∈ {2,000, 6,000, 12,000} tokens × the 2 needles: 273/274 = 2k,
  275/276 = 6k, 277/278 = 12k.
- L is measured with the twins' shared tokenizer, on the bare probe,
  before any config wrapping.
- **Filler:** one fixed public-domain English text, chosen and recorded in
  P (source, edition, hash). It is truncated deterministically, and the
  same filler is used for both needles at each L.
- The 2k probes are a **within-pair control.** Both models should succeed
  there. If they separate the twins at 2k, the separation is not about the
  context limit.

**No probe may be added, removed or edited after this D is approved.** If P
finds a problem with one (e.g. the filler source), it raises a Call.

### Threat-model status

- **F1 and F2** are short and benign: the standard LLMmap setting.
- **F3** assumes the deployment passes a long input through untruncated. An
  application may cap or truncate inputs, which would mask the effect. The
  inputs are also conspicuous and costly.
- F3 results are reported as **deployment-dependent** and never pooled into
  the primary.

## What counts as an answer

**Setup, identical for every arm:**
- **Classifier:** two-way logistic regression (D017 settings: C=1.0,
  lbfgs, max_iter 20000, tol 1e-6, deterministic). Inputs are concatenated
  response embeddings (I5, tok200, unnormalised; no query embedding, which
  is constant for a fixed set). Trained on the twins' S_build traces
  (75 configs × 2).
- **Selection on S_val only, never on S_build statistics.** This is D025's
  lesson: build-statistic argmax selects noise. Greedy forward selection
  minimises S_val mean log-loss of the two-way classifier, nested to
  `k`=8, ties to the lowest id.
- **Evaluation on S_test only:** two-way accuracy over 50 traces.
- **Candidate sets:**
  - **POOL** = the 259 existing queries, from the frozen corpus with no
    regeneration (I6);
  - **NEW** = F1 + F2 (14 short probes);
  - **POOL+NEW** = both.
- **Intervals:** paired config bootstrap on S_test,
  `boot_draws(25, 2000, seed=20261001)`, one set of draws shared by every
  arm.

**Primary contrast:** `Δ = acc_test(POOL+NEW, k=4) − acc_test(POOL, k=4)`.
In words: does adding the short new probes to the pool improve the best
4-query separation of the twins?
- Why `k`=4: a pilot-scale budget, small enough that one strong probe
  shows.
- `k`=1..8 curves are reported descriptively.

**Pre-registered labels** (M = 0.05, same margin and logic as D025):

| label | rule (checked in this order) |
|---|---|
| **CEILING** | `acc_test(POOL, k=4)` > 0.95: the twins are already separable with pool queries by a dedicated classifier, so the GO/NO-GO question does not arise |
| **GO** | `Δ` ≥ +0.05 **and** the lower bound of `Δ` > 0 |
| **NO-GO** | the upper bound of `Δ` < +0.05 |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise |

**Secondary (descriptive):**
- (a) **NEW alone vs POOL alone**, `k`=1..8: raw ammunition, not
  incremental value.
- (b) **Per-probe table:**
  - every new probe's single-query two-way accuracy on S_val and S_test;
  - its byte-identical rate between the twins on their greedy reference
    config (D024 S0 method; the pool's rate for this pair is 46/259);
  - for the selected sets, S_val-minus-S_test optimism.
- (c) **F3 (deployment-dependent):** per-probe accuracy at each L, and
  POOL+NEW+F3 vs POOL+NEW at `k`=4. Also report what each model actually
  outputs at 6k/12k (retrieves the needle / wrong word / degenerate text).
  The 2k control result is reported first.
- (d) **Reference rows:** D025's GLOBAL `k`=8 and FAM:Phi `k`=8 chains,
  re-read under this D's two-way classifier on S_test. They put the D025
  numbers (37-way two-logit) on this D's scale.
- (e) **Hand inspection** (as in T3.1): for the 3 best new probes by
  S_val, quote 2–3 representative responses per model, to show whether the
  separation is semantic (e.g. different stated context lengths) or an
  artefact.
- (f) **Linear meanpool** (D017, C=3.0): the same primary contrast, as a
  readout-sensitivity row.

**Design-side lean, stated before any run:**
- F1/F2 lean **NO-GO or INCONCLUSIVE**. Instruction-tuned models often do
  not know their own context length. But 9/65 helped, so a small gain is
  plausible.
- F3 leans **strong separation at 6k/12k** (the 4k model runs past its
  trained positions) and **none at 2k**.
- CEILING is unlikely: D025's best for this pair under a 37-way-trained
  readout was ≈ .90.

## Required steps

**S0 — Harness parity and set-up (no new probes generated yet).**
- **Parity.** For both twins, regenerate pool queries {9, 65, 140, 193} on
  each model's greedy (`do_sample` = False) configs in S_build, up to 10
  per model, including the D024 tier-1 greedy reference. Use the D006 harness
  (`experiments/d006_s4_shard.py` code path: `LLM_huggingface`, bf16, the
  corpus-wide batch size, 200-token ceiling), at the same HF revisions as
  recorded in the D006 shard status files.
  - Report the byte-identical match rate against `data/corpus_v1`.
  - **Stop and raise a Call if it is < 90%.** A drifted harness would
    confound new-vs-pool.
- **Filler.** Choose and record the F3 filler (source, edition, sha256).
  Report the exact token counts of probes 273–278 under the shared
  tokenizer.
- **Behaviour at > 4k.** Run one 6k probe once on the 4k model and report
  what the harness does (generates / errors / truncates). Do not change
  the harness to "fix" it; whatever it does is the observation.
- **Batch size for F3.** If the corpus-wide batch size OOMs on F3 prompts,
  use a smaller batch for F3 only, identical for both models. D006/S1c
  showed batch size does not shift the response distribution. Disclose it.

**S1 — Generate.**
- All 20 probes × both twins × all 125 configs (each model's own config
  list from `data/corpus_v1`'s `prompt_conf` records, same `config_index`
  → same config): 5,000 generations.
- Store as a **separate, versioned corpus extension**
  (`data/corpus_v1_ext_d026/`), with the same record schema as
  `corpus_v1`. `corpus_v1` is not modified.
- Embed with I5 (tok200, unnormalised), exactly as `d006_s6_embed.py`.
- Commit the generation status and embedding manifest (hashes) before any
  analysis.

**S2 — Analysis.** The primary label, then (a)–(f).

**S3 — `## R`.** The label first, then curves (POOL, NEW, POOL+NEW,
`k`=1..8), the per-probe table, F3, the reference rows, the hand
inspection, and cost.

## Constraints

- **I2.**
  - S_build: training of the two-way classifier.
  - S_val: selection.
  - S_test: evaluation (exploratory label, as in every D since D009).
  - New probes run under each model's existing configs, so the splits are
    unchanged.
- **I5:** same embedding, tok200, unnormalised.
- **I6:** pool responses are read from the frozen corpus, never
  regenerated. The only regeneration is S0's parity check, which is
  compared, not used.
- **I7:** no schema change. The new rows live in a separate versioned
  extension with the same schema. Record the version and a migration note
  in `results/D026/` before S1 writes anything.
- **I1, I3:** unchanged. No tensor or selection statistic is involved in
  the primary, which is classifier-based by design (D025 lesson).
- **No tuning:** C, the selection criterion, `k`=4, M, and the probe list
  are fixed above.
- **Code:** new files only (`experiments/d026_*.py`, slurm files). Import
  the D006 harness; do not edit it.
- **Out of scope:**
  - any model other than the two twins;
  - any probe not listed above;
  - the 37-way pipeline;
  - JointGreedy on an extended pool (that is stage 2, after GO and the
    human).
- **Cost:**
  - 5,000 generations (the 6k/12k probes are prefill-heavy) plus the S0
    parity check;
  - expected ≲ 2 GPU-h;
  - **stop and raise a Call if the projected total exceeds 4 GPU-h**;
  - state the measured cost in `R`.

**S0.**
- **Parity: 40/40 per twin**, on queries 9/65/140/193 × 10 greedy S_build
  configs. Every regenerated config matched on all 259 queries, at the
  recorded revisions, under the current env. No Call.
- **The twins' tokenizers are identical.** Byte-identical greedy answers on
  the reference configs (22 / 56): 46/259, which reproduces D024.
- **Filler:** *Frankenstein*, Project Gutenberg #84, sha256 `7810cd48…`.
  - The source is committed, and the normalisation rule is in the plan.
  - Truncated filler re-tokenises to exactly 2,000 / 6,000 / 12,000 tokens;
    the bare probes are 2,031 / 6,031 / 12,031.
  - Neither needle word occurs in the filler.
- **No `corpus_v1` prompt for either twin exceeds 1,024 tokens**, and
  F1/F2 wrapped prompts reach at most 1,015. The twins' two architectural
  context differences are therefore **inert on the entire existing pool**:
  - the 4k model's 2,047-token sliding window;
  - the 128k model's LongRoPE switch above 4,096.
- **The 4k model's sliding window is live**: 12.8 max logit shift at 3,000
  tokens when disabled, with the argmax changed; 0.0 at 1,500.
- **The 2k probes cross the window:** 114/125 of them exceed 2,047 tokens
  after config wrapping.
- **The 4k model at 6k:** it generates without error (a length warning only)
  and answers `"Wretchedness"`, a wrong word.
- **Re-batching changes ~30% of greedy outputs.** The same 4 queries
  regenerated alone match only 70% / 67.5%: bf16 padding sensitivity.

**Calls for design side.**
1. **F3 batching** (departs from the D's "only on OOM" wording).
   - The 128k model's RoPE regime is set by the longest sequence in the
     batch, so mixing lengths moves short probes into long-factor mode.
   - **Recommend:** F1+F2 as one batch of 14 per config; F3 as one call per
     L holding its two needle probes. These have identical lengths, so there
     is zero padding (asserted), with batch 1 as the fallback.
2. **Needle text.** Read `*marmalade*` as Markdown emphasis, so the probe is
   the plain `The secret word is marmalade.` (flag in `d026_lib`).
3. **The 2k probes are not a clean control.**
   - Recommend re-labelling them "window-boundary" in R and reporting them
     split by wrapped length (≤ / > 2,047 tokens).
   - Optionally, design-side adds a real 1k control by amending `## D`.
   - It bears on F3's interpretation, so the human may want to see it.
4. **Disclosures:**
   - per-config sampling seeds;
   - batch-sensitivity is symmetric between the twins;
   - revision pin;
   - an in-job 4 GPU-h projection guard;
   - fixed F3 output-class rule;
   - `.gitignore` for the extension shard.
   - Logged as `TACC_NOTES` Issue 20.

**Cost.**

- S1: expected ≈ 1.5–2.5 GPU-h, worst case ≈ 3.6, under the 4 GPU-h Call
  threshold.
