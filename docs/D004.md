## D — The question

> **Under a two-sample statistic that (a) compares point cloud vs point
> cloud rather than trace vs collapsed centroid (invariant I3), and (b) has
> enough resolution not to saturate at a ceiling — does the "heavy
> exploitable hard tail" premise (`METHOD.md §2`, invariant I1) still hold,
> or does it disappear once the measurement artifact from D001 is removed?**

### Why this exists — D001's result was split, and the negative half is suspect

D001 found two things that came apart cleanly:

1. **Structure is real.** Near-relative model pairs are significantly harder
   to separate than unrelated pairs (Mann-Whitney p = 3.5e-32). Not in
   question; D004 does not re-test this.
2. **The "heavy tail" verdict came back negative — but the instrument that
   produced it was flawed in a specific, diagnosable way** (D001 `## R`
   §R2): it compared individual traces against a **collapsed centroid**
   (violates I3 — templates are single averaged vectors, per
   `LLMmap/templates.py:58`), it measured a **2-way** distinction where the
   real task is **52-way**, and 88.2% of pairs hit an exact resolution
   ceiling (`AUC = 1.0` = "zero errors in 4624 comparisons," not "provably
   perfectly separable").

D001 itself could not test the tail premise cleanly, because the shipped
artifacts only expose a collapsed centroid — this is not a new concern, it
is D001's own R6 flagging that its own instrument was unfit for the
question it was asked, and recommending exactly this follow-up.

### Why this is a REVIEW-gated escalation, not a routine follow-up

Two outcomes are possible and they point in materially different
directions for the project:

- If the tail reappears under a fair instrument → D001's negative result
  was an artifact, `METHOD.md` and I1 stand as written, proceed to Phase 1.
- If the tail still does not appear under a fair, non-saturating instrument
  → per D001 R6, **that is a genuine falsification** and escalates from
  "measurement problem" to "the CDQD premise needs revision" — squarely
  `CLAUDE.md`'s escalation category (a) invariant-touching and (d)
  METHOD.md-revising. This D produces the evidence; it does not itself
  decide which branch is taken — that stays a human call once R lands.

### Prior expectation, pre-registered

We expect the tail to reappear once measured fairly — D001's own R2
attributes the saturation to the instrument (centroid collapse + 2-way
framing + resolution floor), not to genuine model similarity, and the
*structural* result (premise 2) already shows the same near-relative pairs
driving separability difficulty under the flawed instrument. If the tail
does **not** reappear under a proper instrument, that contradicts this
expectation and is the more consequential of the two outcomes — flag it
prominently rather than downplaying it.

## What counts as an answer

| Outcome | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **TAIL CONFIRMED** | Under the I3-compliant statistic (M1) and the k-way statistic (M2), the CVaR/mean ratio is meaningfully below 1 (not pinned near the ceiling the way D001's M6=0.9966 was), **and** the distribution shows genuine spread (not >80% of mass at one value). D001's negative result is attributed to its instrument; `METHOD.md`/I1 stand. |
| **TAIL STILL ABSENT (genuine falsification)** | Ratio stays ≈1 under both M1 and M2, **and** there is evidence the statistics have room to show spread (i.e. they are not themselves saturating in the same way — report the full distribution, not just the ratio). Per D001 R6, this escalates to the human as a `METHOD.md`/I1 revision question — D004 reports it, does not resolve it. |
| **STILL INCONCLUSIVE** | The new statistics also saturate, or CIs span no-effect. Report the distribution and exactly where/why the new instrument still hits a ceiling; do not force a verdict, and flag what a third instrument would need to differ on. |

## Required measurements

**M1 — I3-compliant pairwise statistic.** Per-pair separability computed as
a point-cloud-vs-point-cloud statistic on the **individual trace
embeddings** (not the collapsed `templates.json` centroids) — the A3
default candidate (`DECISIONS.md`): 5-fold CV AUC of a linear probe trained
to distinguish model A's traces from model B's traces. Report the same
distribution shape D001 reported (mean, percentiles, CVaR₀.₁, M6 ratio) so
the two are directly comparable.

**M2 — A k-way statistic reflecting the actual 52-way task.** D001 R2 point
1: pairwise separability is a fundamentally easier question than the real
52-way classification task. Compute an open-set top-1 confusion measure per
model (e.g. nearest-neighbor-by-point-cloud classification accuracy, or
per-class recall in a linear multiclass probe over all 52 models on the
same trace embeddings) and derive a per-*pair* confusion rate from the
resulting confusion matrix (how often is A misclassified as B or vice
versa, among all 52 candidates). Report its own distribution and CVaR/mean
ratio.

**M3 — Resolution check on both M1 and M2.** Explicitly report what
fraction of pairs sit at a measurement ceiling or floor for each statistic,
mirroring D001's finding that 88.2% sat at exactly AUC=1.0. If either
statistic still saturates heavily, say so plainly — do not let a ratio
near 1 stand in for "no tail" if the underlying distribution is still
censored.

**M4 — The motivating case, carried forward.** Report Llama-3-70B ↔
Smaug-70B's percentile rank under M1 and M2, alongside D001's 0.75
(open-set nearest-template) for continuity.

**M5 — Data provenance check.** State explicitly whether this D reused
D001's cached trace embeddings (per D001/P1 §S1, cached to `.npy`) or had
to re-extract them, and why. `.npy` caches are excluded from git
(`.gitignore`) and may live only on TACC's scratch filesystem, which is
purged on a timer (flagged independently in the project's verification
notes) — check before assuming the cache is still there.

## Constraints

**Applicable invariants:** I1 and I3 are the direct subject of this D.
I6 (frozen corpus/tensor for comparisons) applies in spirit: **reuse
D001's already-embedded trace data if still present rather than
re-collecting from the models** — this is a re-*analysis* of existing
embeddings under new statistics, not a new data-collection effort, and
should cost close to nothing if the cache survived. If the cache did not
survive, re-extracting embeddings from the same shipped test set (no new
model generation, no new hyperparameters) is still acceptable and should
still be cheap — flag which case applies (M5).

**Out of scope:** do not build the real Phase-1 corpus, do not implement
`GreedyCover` or the full separability tensor across the eventual `Q_pool`.
This D re-measures the *existing* D001 premise question with a better
instrument — nothing more.

**Keep this D's own cost small,** consistent with D001/D002's precedent —
this is a pilot re-measurement, not a new large experiment.

### P1

**Status: PROPOSED — awaiting review.** Full plan: [`plans/D004-P1.md`](plans/D004-P1.md).

**M5 is already answered, and D004's assumption about it is off.** D004's M5 note
says the `.npy` caches "may live only on TACC's scratch filesystem, which is purged
on a timer." They do not — they are under `results/D001/` on **`$WORK`** (Lustre,
**no purge policy**). Only *model weights* moved to `$SCRATCH` (D002/P1.a). The
cache is intact: `f_test.npy` = 3536 × 384 float32, i.e. exactly the 68 individual
trace embeddings per model × 52 models, never averaged. **So this is a pure
re-analysis: no re-extraction, no generation, no GPU.**

**Pre-registered prediction: the A3-default probe will probably saturate too.**
`f_test` is the *output of the contrastively-trained fingerprinting network*, so a
linear probe on 384-d with 68+68 points per pair will likely hit CV AUC ≈ 1.0 for
most pairs — reproducing D001's ceiling in new coordinates rather than escaping it.
Stated now so it is falsifiable.

**That prediction is why P1 adds an unbounded companion statistic rather than
waiting to return INCONCLUSIVE.** Plan measures three things:

- **M1a** — A3 default: 5-fold CV AUC of an L2-regularized linear probe, point-cloud
  vs point-cloud (I3-compliant). Bounded; kept for direct comparability with D001.
- **M1b** — **energy distance** between clouds (sanctioned by `DECISIONS.md` A3 as a
  robustness check), raw and scale-free. **Unbounded, so it cannot saturate** and
  preserves ordering among pairs that AUC pins at 1.0. A CVaR/mean ratio on a
  *censored* variable is uninterpretable — that is exactly what went wrong in D001 —
  so this is the statistic that actually carries the answer.
- **M2** — 52-way: leave-one-out nearest-neighbour over point clouds → 52×52
  confusion → per-pair rates, with a 52-class multiclass probe as a second opinion.
  Chosen as primary because it is non-parametric and is the cloud analogue of
  LLMmap's own nearest-template inference. D001's baseline was Top-1 ≈ 88–91% over
  52 classes, so M2 has real error mass to distribute by construction.

**M3 resolution audit is treated as a gate, not a footnote:** a ratio near 1 will
**not** be reported as "no tail" unless the audit shows the distribution is not
censored.

**Cost.** Re-analysis of a 5.4 MB cached array — no GPU, no model loading. One
`gh-dev` job, `-t 00:30:00`, expected a few minutes. Only new dependency:
`scikit-learn` (not currently installed).

**Limitation carried forward (see P1 §5 and the TACC note in D001's `## Review`):**
D004 inherits D001's traces, and whether the shipped dataset's train/test
prompt-config holdout was ever applied is unverifiable from the artifact — while a
live bug in the current generator (`prompt_configuration.py:239`) means the holdout
is *not* applied today. If the shipped dataset shares that defect it inflates every
statistic here and in D001 alike — a fourth candidate cause of saturation beyond
D001 R2's three. It does not change D004's *comparative* conclusion (all instruments
run on the same traces) but must be stated in R.
