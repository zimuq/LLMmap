## Why this D

- **Facts on record (old S_test, `k` = 8, attention):**
  - **v2:** MMR-PP[CVaR] (λ = 0.7, Pearson) beats JG by +5.8 pp [+4.1, +7.5]; linear +1.5 pp (D031). It also leads
    on H_all (0.900 vs 0.868) and on held-out models (D033).
  - **v1:** PARITY (+0.0 pp); linear leans JG.
  - **Chain overlap:** JG ∩ MMR\* is 7/8 on v1 and 3/8 on v2. On v2, MMR\* ∩ TOP-K[CVaR] is 5/8.
- **Redundancy is not the obvious explanation.** At `k` = 8 on v2 (D037 (c), S_build pair profiles), the
  within-set Pearson correlation is JG 0.119, MMR\* 0.178, TOP-K[CVaR] 0.372. JG with CVaR is already the
  least redundant set; it is only JG with the mean that stacks duplicates (0.813).
- **Three candidate explanations, all observable on S_build:**
  - **H-strength.** MMR keeps individually strong queries; its relevance term is each query's own CVaR₀.₁.
    JG, conditioning every step on the current set's hardest pairs, picks individually weaker queries.
  - **H-redundancy.** MMR's sets carry less shared information than JG's on some measure other than mean
    pairwise correlation. This is expected to fail, given the numbers above, but is checked for completeness.
  - **H-chase.** JG's later steps chase the residual hardest pairs, including pairs the pool cannot separate
    (D028: no single pool query separates the tiny-aya regional variants, granite-3.2 vs 3.1, or Qwen3.5-4B vs
    9B). Its gains on those pairs are partly S_build noise that does not transfer: the winner's curse of D011 /
    D025, now inside a greedy loop. MMR's relevance averages over all 357 bottom-decile pairs per query and is
    steadier.
- **The test that separates them is the "two halves" design (M3).** Split each model's S_build configs into two
  disjoint halves, select on one, measure on the other. Noise-chasing shows up as optimism: a set that looks good
  on the half it was chosen on, and worse on the half it was not.
- **v1 is the negative control.** JG and MMR\* nearly coincide on v1, so any mechanism that explains v2 should be
  weak or absent there.

## D — The question

> **On S_build only, for v2 and v1: which of query strength (H-strength),
> redundancy (H-redundancy) and tail-chasing of noise (H-chase) separates
> JointGreedy-CVaR's 8-query set from CVaR + MMR's?**

### Rules diagnosed

| rule | selection call (unchanged code) | reselected on halves (M3, M4)? |
|---|---|---|
| **JG** | `LLMmap/joint_greedy.joint_greedy(..., gamma=0.1)` on the S_build accumulators | yes |
| **MMR\*** | `d031_lib.mmr(Esf, 0.7, "pearson")`: D031's S_val choice `MMR\|pearson\|0.7` in both views (`results/D031/variant_choice.json`) | yes |
| **TOP-K[CVaR]** | `d031_lib.topk(Esf)` | yes |
| **GC** | `LLMmap/greedy_cover.greedy_cover(S, 8, gamma=0.1, agg="max")` | yes (v2; v1 only if S0 reproduces a stored v1 GC chain) |
| MMR λ = 0.3, 0.5 (Pearson) | `d031_lib.mmr` | full S_build only, descriptive (M1, M2) |
| PAPER8 | fixed | evaluated only (no selection) |

- `Esf` / `S` for a config subset C are the per-query scale-free energies computed from the accumulators
  restricted to C (see "Two-halves mechanics" below). On full S_build they must reproduce the frozen tensors
  (S0).
- `k` = 1..8 nested; ties go to the lowest id, as in every rule's own code.

### Two-halves mechanics (M3, M4)

- **Splits.** R = 20 random splits, seeds 20261109 + r. Each model's 75 S_build configs (both views; S0 asserts
  the count) are split by index into A (37) and B (38).
  - Configs are drawn per model and are not shared across models (TACC_NOTES Issue 18), so an index split is a
    valid per-model split.
  - Each split is used in both directions (select on A, measure on B, and vice versa), giving **40 directed
    splits**.
- **No recomputation of embeddings.** XY2 / XX2 hold per-query config × config squared-distance blocks
  (`d010_select.precompute`), so a half is a sub-block `[..., C, C]`. Every statistic on a half is computed from
  the restricted blocks with the frozen float32 operations.
- **Nothing here reads S_val or S_test.**

### Measures

**M1 — query strength (H-strength).**
- A query's strength on config subset C is `s_q(C)` = CVaR₀.₁ over all pairs of its single-query scale-free
  energy on C. This is exactly the quantity TOP-K ranks by and MMR uses as relevance (`d031_lib.single_cvar`).
- Per chain at `k` = 8, report:
  - the mean `s_q` of its queries;
  - their mean pool rank (1 = strongest of 259);
  - the per-step `s_q` of each pick.
- On full S_build, every chain is reported. On halves, for chains selected on A, `s_q` is measured on B
  (held-out strength).
- Also reported: the mean held-out strength of **JG's picks not in MMR\*'s set** against **MMR\*'s picks not
  in JG's**. On v2 at full S_build these are 5 vs 5 queries.

**M2 — redundancy (H-redundancy).**
- Within-set pair-profile Pearson and cosine at `k` = 8 (the D032 / D037 measure, `d032_s0.within`).
- Effective number of queries: (Σλ)² / Σλ², from the eigenvalues λ of the 8 × 8 pair-profile correlation
  matrix. 8 means no shared information; 1 means a single query repeated.
- Per step: the pick's maximum correlation with earlier picks.
- Reported on full S_build and held-out (B profiles for A-selected chains).

**M3 — transfer and tail-chasing (H-chase), the two-halves test.** For every directed split (select on A,
measure on B) and every reselected rule:
- **Optimism** on two yardsticks, each computed for the rule's chain from A:
  - Y_joint = JG's own objective (CVaR₀.₁ over pairs of the set's joint scale-free energy);
  - Y_max = GC's objective (CVaR₀.₁ over pairs of MAX coverage).
  - Optimism = Y(chain_A on A) − Y(chain_A on B). It is the part of a set's apparent quality that was specific
    to the configs it was chosen on.
  - Report each rule's optimism and its held-out level Y(chain_A on B).
- **Per-step optimism** for JG and MMR\*: the step-`j` marginal gain in Y_joint, on A vs on B, for `j` = 1..8.
- **Chain stability:** |chain_A ∩ chain_B| at `k` = 8 and `k` = 4.
- **What JG's later steps chase.** At JG steps `j` = 4..8 on A, take the pairs in the current set's bottom
  decile (the pairs the step is optimising). Report:
  - the share of those pairs with low pool ceiling, where pool ceiling c_p = max over the 259 queries of the
    single-query energy on **B**, and "low" = bottom decile of c_p over all pairs;
  - the share in H_all (D028) and in N′ (D027);
  - the same shares for MMR\*'s set at the same `k`, as a reference.

**M4 — build-internal proxy accuracy (sanity anchor).**
- Readout ② raw (D033's frozen point-to-cloud 2A − W rule), with reference clouds from A and probes = each
  B-config of each model, summed over the chain's queries.
- Endpoints at `k` = 1..8:
  - top-1 over the view's models (P_all);
  - CVaR₀.₁ over all pairs of the two-model accuracy (P_tail), as D033's O_tail but on S_build halves.
- Chains: each reselected rule's chain_A, plus PAPER8.
- **Purpose:** checks whether the S_build-internal picture reproduces the known old-S_test ordering
  (v2: MMR\* > JG; v1: ≈). It is a sanity anchor, not an outcome.

### Pre-registered readings

All comparisons are paired across the 40 directed splits. These splits share configs and are **not**
independent, so the spread is reported as a consistency band, not a confidence interval.
- "Consistent" means the paired difference has the same sign in **≥ 36 of 40** directed splits.
- The band is the 2.5–97.5 percentile of the 40 values.

| hypothesis | supported on v2 if | negative control (v1) |
|---|---|---|
| **H-strength** | MMR\* − JG in mean held-out strength (M1) > 0, consistent | difference smaller in magnitude than v2's |
| **H-redundancy** | JG's held-out effective number (M2) < MMR\*'s, consistent | — |
| **H-chase** | JG's optimism on Y_joint > MMR\*'s **and** > TOP-K[CVaR]'s, both consistent; **and** JG's late-step chased pairs have a higher low-ceiling share than MMR\*'s bottom decile at the same `k` | JG − MMR\* optimism difference smaller than v2's |

- **Sanity anchor (M4):**
  - If P_all at `k` = 8 does **not** order MMR\* above JG on v2 (paired median difference ≤ 0), R says so, and
    the hypothesis readings are reported as "S_build-internal only, does not reproduce the test ordering".
  - That outcome is not a stop.

**What each outcome leads to (design-side, for the follow-up D):**

| outcome | follow-up candidate(s) |
|---|---|
| H-chase supported | a **cross-fitted JointGreedy** (gains computed on one half, verified on the other), plus **SUM-coverage + CVaR** |
| H-strength supported, H-chase not | **SUM-coverage + CVaR**: it accumulates every query's evidence, so strong queries keep their value, and a redundant query cannot lift the bottom decile |
| both | both candidates |
| neither, or M4 fails the anchor | no new selection rule. MMR's v2 edge is recorded as not explained by S_build-observable properties, and JG stays. The SUM comparison goes ahead only if wanted for I4. |

- **The method is not changed by this D.** It reads no target data and labels nothing as BETTER / WORSE.

### Secondary (descriptive)

- (a) The full-S_build M1 / M2 table for all rules, including MMR λ = 0.3 / 0.5. This shows where each λ sits
  between strength and diversity.
- (b) The M3 per-step optimism curves for JG, MMR\*, TOP-K[CVaR] and GC, both views.
- (c) The query ids of JG's and MMR\*'s non-shared picks on full S_build v2, with each query's text category
  (from the pool metadata) and its M1 strength.

  not even for sanity.
