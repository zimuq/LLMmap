## Why this D

- **S_test is used up.** D029–D032 read the current S_test many times, and
  the headline rule (CVaR + MMR) was adopted after D031 read it. Every
  number on it is now exploratory.
- **Fresh configs need new prompts and settings.** I2 is per parameter, and
  every existing component is already allocated: 84 system prompts
  (42 / 21 / 21), CoT prompts (3 / 3 / 3), RAG templates (4 / 5 / 5), and
  temperatures (0.1–1.0, split across pools).
- **Generation is small.** Only the queries in the frozen chains need
  answers (a union of a few dozen of the 259), for all 85 models.

## D — The question

> **On a new, never-used split of deployment configs (S_test′), with every
> chain, variant and readout recipe frozen beforehand, does CVaR + MMR
> (pair-profile, pearson | 0.7) identify better than (1) LLMmap's 8
> queries, (2) the mean criterion, and (3) JointGreedy?**

### Arms (all chains frozen; nothing is re-selected)

| arm | chain | role |
|---|---|---|
| **OURS** | MMR-PP[CVaR] \| pearson \| 0.7 (D031 / D032 star; v2 and v1 chains) | method |
| **PAPER8** | LLMmap's 8 queries | primary 1 |
| **TOP-K[MEAN]** | D032 chain | primary 2 (the criterion claim) |
| **JG** | D029 (v2) / D010 (v1) chain | primary 3 |
| TOP-K[CVaR] | D031 chain | row (the simplest alternative rule) |
| MMR-TXT[CVaR]\* | D032 chain | row (Q2 on fresh configs) |
| D033 fold chains | `results/D033/chains.json` | open-set section only |

- `k` = 1–8 for every closed-set arm (curves); labels at `k` = 8.
- **External baselines (included; human, 2026-10-22): MET and ZeroPrint**
  under D030's native protocols (prompts, sampling, statistic, readout from
  their official code; our renderer).
  - **Only the target side is new.** Targets are generated under S_test′
    configs with each method's own prompts. Reference libraries (Gen, All
    from S_build configs) are D030's, reused verbatim.
  - **Variant frozen beforehand:** each method's label variant is D030's
    strongest (D030 R B\*: MET-e5 | All in both views; ZeroPrint ZP-512 | All on
    v2, ZP-200 | All on v1 — one generation, two truncations). It was chosen
    on the old S_test, which favours them, and this is stated in R. No
    re-choice on S_test′.
  - **Number of S_test′ configs per model for their targets:** all 25 by
    default (D030: MET all 25, T_ZP = 25). At stop A TACC reports the
    measured cost. If it is prohibitive, TACC may propose a subset fixed
    before generation.
  - **Labels:** Δ = OURS − X as D030, on the **unselected** endpoints only
    (E_all, all-pair E_tail; no H_all, D030 lesson B1).

### S_test′ — new configs (built in P1, frozen before generation)

- **Size and structure as S_test:** 25 configs per model, sampled by D006 /
  S3's procedure (`general.json`: COT_P, RAG_P, WITH_SYSTEM_P, RAG chunk
  range), from **new component pools**:
  - ≥ 21 new system prompts;
  - 3 new CoT prompts;
  - 5 new RAG templates;
  - temperature values not in any existing pool.
  - `do_sample` stays shared (A5 carve-out, `general.json` note).
- **Disjointness checks (I2):**
  - exact-string disjointness from every existing pool;
  - no near-duplicates: each new prompt's max e5 cosine to every existing
    prompt is reported, with a pre-declared cap that P1 proposes;
  - no new temperature equals an existing one.
- **Source of the new prompts** is a P1 Call (e.g. an unused public
  system-prompt collection, or a proxy LLM under a fixed instruction, D003
  precedent), with the author and procedure recorded.
- **Naming:** the corpus stays **corpus v2** (85 models; v1's 37 are a
  subset view). S_test′ is a new split added to corpus v2, not a new
  dataset. "dataset_all" is not used (human, 2026-10-22).
- **Corpus version bump (I7)** before generation: S_test′ becomes a new split
  (`split_v3` or equivalent), with a migration note. Existing splits stay
  byte-identical.

### Generation and embedding (P1, after stop A)

- 85 models × 25 S_test′ configs × the **union** of:
  - the closed-set arms' `k` ≤ 8 chains, both views;
  - D033's fold chains.
- Same renderer, truncation (tok200) and frozen e5 embedding as corpus v2,
  including LLMmap's upstream BOS behaviour (Issue 24, kept for
  consistency).
- **Readouts are not retrained on new data.** They are trained on S_build
  exactly as in D031 / D032 (same seeds; reproduction asserted) and only
  scored on S_test′.

## What counts as an answer

**Endpoints** (S_test′, `k` = 8, per view): **E_all** (top-1; 85-way v2,
37-way v1) and **E_tail** (CVaR₀.₁ of two-logit accuracy over **all**
pairs). Bootstrap: D023 hierarchical config × seed, 2,000 replicates, new
draws over S_test′ configs seeded in P1. Labels as D031 (δ = 0.02).

**Primaries (pre-registered, approved by the human 2026-10-22):**

| # | Δ | claim if BETTER |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | OURS − PAPER8 | our selection beats LLMmap's published queries on unseen deployments |
| 2 | OURS − TOP-K[MEAN] | the tail criterion's advantage holds on unseen deployments |
| 3 | OURS − JG | CVaR + MMR is the better combination rule |

- **Label readout:** to be fixed by the human **before stop B** (open
  decision: attention vs linear vs "both must agree"). If no decision is
  recorded by stop B, the D029–D032 convention applies: attention labels,
  linear agreeing in sign for BETTER. It can't be chosen after scores exist.
- **v2 primary; v1 equal prominence;** no cross-view level comparison.
- No multiplicity correction (project convention). Every primary is
  reported, with WORSE first.

**Open-set section** (P2, after D033 closes): D033's frozen protocol (folds,
② variant, ① recipe and checkpoints) re-scored on S_test′. Same endpoints
(O_all, O_tail) and D033's claim rule.

**Secondary (descriptive):**
- (a) Rows TOP-K[CVaR] and MMR-TXT[CVaR]\*; curves `k` = 1–8.
- (b) **Exploratory-vs-confirmatory table:** each primary's Δ on the old
  S_test (D031 / D032 / S6) next to S_test′. Shrinkage is reported, not
  interpreted.
- (c) Near-relative breakdown (N′ / D001 pairs); the mandatory hard-tail
  plot (I1).
- (d) Per-config-family breakdown (system prompt / RAG / CoT / sampling),
  as D030.

## Constraints

- **I2:** S_test′ components disjoint from build / val / test at the
  parameter level. Selection and variant choices are not revisited:
  everything is frozen from D031–D033. **S_test′ is read once, in P2.**
- **I3 / I5 / I6:** frozen e5, same embedding pipeline; chains, readout
  recipes and seeds unchanged.
- **I7:** version bump + migration note committed **before** generation.
- **I4** untouched. **I1:** hard-tail plot on S_test′.
- **No re-selection, no new variants, no tuning on S_test′.** If something
  forces a change after stop B, it is post-hoc and labelled so.
- **Out of scope:** new models; rejection (open-set (b)); D033's transfer
  questions beyond its frozen protocol.
- **Cost (design-side guess):**
  - ours: generation ≈ 85 × 25 × |union| (≈ 40–60 queries) ≈ 85k–130k
    answers;
  - MET / ZeroPrint targets: ≈ 85 × 25 × (250 + ZeroPrint's per-config
    budget) answers, the largest share. D030's target side is the
    reference for cost;
  - plus embedding and readout retraining, comparable to D031 / D032.
  - Measured at stop A. **Call if > 150 node-h** in total before generation.
