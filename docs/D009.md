## D — The question

> **Training LLmap's own stage-2 pipeline from scratch — the frozen I5
> embedding plus a trained projection and self-attention siamese/
> classifier network (`LLMmap/inference_model_archs.py`,
> `LLMmap/trainer.py`) — once per candidate query strategy, holding the
> training procedure identical and varying only which `k` queries built
> the training corpus: does CVaR-coverage selection produce measurably
> better closed-set accuracy than mean-greedy selection, random
> selection, or the paper's own 8 queries, at equal `k`?**

This is the fair-comparison protocol `METHOD.md §5.5` specifies. Nothing
about the tensor, the corpus, or the selection algorithm changes here —
this D asks whether the proxy-metric gains D008 already found (large,
robust query-*efficiency* win; mixed worst-class picture) survive
contact with the classifier the paper and any reviewer actually cares
about, or whether they are an artifact of the cheap stand-in used during
selection.

### Why this exists now, not later

D008 closed with a real but partial picture: CVaR-coverage's advantage
on the tensor-level proxy is large and statistically robust on mean
top-1 and query efficiency, genuinely mixed on worst-class, and the
"selection missed an already-good pool query" finding (33/37 pairs)
raised a real question about whether Phase 3 (targeted generation) is
even the right next investment. **Before deciding that, or refining
selection further, the human decided to check whether any of this
translates once the actual paper pipeline is trained** — a proxy-metric
win that doesn't survive a real classifier would redirect effort
entirely differently than one that does.

### What's already decided and does not block this D

- **A4** (query efficiency is the primary claim): ✅ decided,
  2026-09-08. This D reports all four `METHOD.md §8` metrics regardless
  — it is what makes the primary-claim numbers real rather than proxy.
- **Which query sets to train on:** fixed. Reuse
  `results/D008/selection.json` verbatim — `cvar_max` (γ=0.1),
  `mean_greedy_max` (γ=1.0), `paper8`, and `random` (D008's own draws or
  a smaller re-draw, see Call 2). **Do not re-run `GreedyCover` or
  recompute the tensor** — I6 applies to the *selection outputs* here,
  not just the raw tensor.
- **Trace format:** fixed by `METHOD.md §5.5`, verified directly against
  `LLMmap/inference.py:92–113` — `trace = [E(query) ; E(response)]`,
  2048-d, concatenated in embedding space (not text space), one per
  query slot, stacked into a length-`k` sequence. **This project's
  corpus currently stores response embeddings only** (`D006-P1.md` S6) —
  embedding the selected query texts is part of S1 below, not already
  done.
- **Training code:** fixed. Reuse `LLMmap/trainer.py`
  (`LLMmapTrainerClosed`) and `LLMmap/inference_model_archs.py`
  (`InferenceModelLLMmap`, `make_siamese_network`) unmodified — do not
  write a new training loop. "Hold the training procedure identical"
  means: same `hparams` dict (optimizer, LR, epochs, architecture size)
  across every strategy and every `k`; only the training data (which
  queries' traces) changes.

## What counts as an answer

| Outcome | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **CONFIRMED** | CVaR-coverage shows meaningfully better closed-set accuracy than mean-greedy at equal `k`, tracking the *shape* (not necessarily the magnitude) of D008's proxy-metric finding — large efficiency gain at small `k`, genuinely mixed worst-class picture is an acceptable sub-result here, not a failure. The headline claim (query efficiency, A4) survives contact with a real classifier. |
| **FALSIFIED (proxy metric was misleading)** | CVaR-coverage ≈ mean-greedy ≈ random once trained, despite D008's tensor-level gap. **This is the single most important possible outcome of this D** — it would mean the entire proxy-metric-driven comparison (D004–D008) does not predict what matters, and the project's remaining direction (Phase 3, any further selection-algorithm work) needs to be reconsidered before any of it proceeds. Do not treat this as this D having failed. |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | Mixed or noisy signal not attributable to a clear cause (e.g. training instability, too few epochs, a config bug) — distinguish this explicitly from FALSIFIED, which requires the training itself to be verified sound (see S4's sanity check). |

evaluation (S8 below) is included as a **supplementary, disclosed
measurement** — it does **not** factor into CONFIRMED/FALSIFIED/
INCONCLUSIVE above, which stay closed-set-only exactly as written.

## Required steps

**S1 — Embed the selected query texts.** For every query index appearing
in any of the four conditions' `k=1..8` chains (`results/D008/selection.json`),
compute `E(query)` with the same cached I5 model used for the corpus
(`multilingual-e5-large-instruct`). Cheap (≤ a few dozen static strings,
no new generation), but store it as a first-class artifact
(`results/D009/query_embeddings.npy` + an index by pool position) rather
than recomputing it inline per run.

**S2 — Build per-strategy, per-`k` training/val/test tensors.** For each
condition × `k`, assemble `[E(query) ; E(response)]` traces (S1's query
embeddings + the already-stored response embeddings) for every model ×
config in `S_build`/`S_val`/`S_test` (I2), restricted to that strategy's
selected `k` queries. **Call 1 (flagged for design-side review):**
propose the concrete `k` values to actually train (the full `k=1..8`
grid × 4 conditions is 32 training runs — state a measured per-run cost
from a pilot before committing to the full grid; a representative subset
such as `k∈{1,2,4,8}` may be sufficient to characterize the curve, but
this is a real trade-off between coverage and cost, not a default to
assume).

**S3 — Train.** For each condition × chosen `k`, instantiate
`InferenceModelLLMmap` + `LLMmapTrainerClosed` with an **identical**
`hparams` dict (state its source: the released repo's own shipped
defaults if any exist, or a stated, disclosed choice if not) and train
on that strategy's `S_build` traces, validating on `S_val`, early-stopping
per the trainer's own logic. **Call 2 (flagged):** propose how many
random-`k` seeds to actually train — D008's proxy metric used 200 seeds
because a nearest-neighbor classifier is nearly free; training a real
network per seed is not. A small number (e.g. 3–5) with a stated
variance-band rationale is the likely answer, but state and justify it
rather than inheriting D008's 200.

**S4 — Sanity-check training before trusting any comparison.** Before
reporting condition-vs-condition deltas, confirm training actually
worked: train/val accuracy curves converge (not diverging or flat at
chance), and **the paper's own 8-query condition at `k=8` should land in
the neighborhood of a sensible closed-set accuracy** (not necessarily
95.35% — `METHOD.md §6.2`'s three-way-split caveat applies — but not
chance-level either). If it doesn't, the training setup itself is
suspect and results are INCONCLUSIVE, not FALSIFIED.

**S5 — Evaluate all four `METHOD.md §8` metrics on `S_test`** (touched
once per I2), for every condition × `k` trained. Report mean top-1,
worst-class, hard-subset (same pair definition D008 Call 2 established:
our own hardest near-relative pairs, headlined by
`Falcon3-10B↔Falcon3-7B` — not the paper's literal Llama-3-70B/Smaug
pair, which is out of universe per A1), and queries-needed-to-reach-X%.

**S6 — Compare against D008's proxy-metric numbers directly**, condition
by condition, at every `k` trained. State plainly whether the *direction*
and *rough magnitude* of D008's findings survive (CONFIRMED-leaning) or
do not (FALSIFIED-leaning) — this comparison is the actual point of the
D, not a footnote.

**S7 — Write `## R`:** verdict per the outcome table above, all four
metrics per condition per `k`, the trained-vs-proxy comparison (S6),
training sanity-check results (S4), and explicit statement of Call 1/2
dispositions and the `hparams` source.

**S8 — Open-set evaluation (approved 2026-09-09; TACC's F7, priced in
`P1` at ≤46 min/run).** At `k=8` only, for all four conditions: train
the paper's open-set/contrastive variant (the pre-head features
`templates.py`'s nearest-template approach needs — a different training
objective from the closed-set softmax network, per F7's own finding
that this is not a free second head on the closed network). Reuse the
same `[E(query);E(response)]` traces (S1/S2) and split discipline (I2)
as S3. Report using whatever evaluation metric the paper's own open-set
approach implies (state precisely which — e.g. nearest-template
top-1/top-k accuracy) in `results/D009/open_set_metrics.json`. **This is
a supplementary deliverable, per the note above the outcome table — do
not fold its result into S7's verdict.**
