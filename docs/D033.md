---

## Why this D

- LLMmap sells open-set identification. AdaptPrint and other identification
  papers report it, and reviewers of a paper built on LLMmap will ask
  (TODO T6.4, D030 reading).
- Our **selection is classifier-free** (it only reads the S_build tensor);
  only the readouts are closed-set. Open-set therefore needs a different
  readout, not a different method.
- D030 found LLMmap's released open-set encoder drops from 0.72 on models it
  was trained on to 0.40 on unseen ones. Whether that gap is the encoder's
  or the query set's is open.
- **Held-out models must also be excluded from selection.** Otherwise the
  query set has already "seen" them. This makes the D a second question:
  do selected queries transfer to models they were not chosen on?

---

## D — The question

> **With some models held out of everything — query selection and readout
> training — and enrolled only from their S_build-config responses, how
> accurately are targets from those models identified among all enrolled
> models? Does our selection (CVaR + MMR on pair-profiles, chosen without
> the held-out models) beat LLMmap's own 8 queries under each of two
> open-set readouts, and how much does excluding the held-out models from
> selection cost?**

### Folds (fixed in S0, before any tensor statistic is read)

- **Primary — model-level folds, family-stratified.** v2: 85 models → 5
  folds of 17. Stratify by family (the D001 / D027 base-and-lineage rule),
  so that families with ≥ 2 members have members on both sides wherever
  possible. A held-out model often has an enrolled sibling, which is the
  realistic hard case ("a new fine-tune of a known base").
- **Secondary (descriptive) — family-level folds.** Whole families held out
  together, ≈ 5 folds of similar size.
- **v1 view:** the same assignment restricted to v1's 37 models (no separate
  draw).
- Assignment seeded `default_rng(sha256("D033|folds|<kind>"))`. Folds and
  family labels are committed in `chains.json`.

### Selection arms (per fold, S_build only, on pairs among that fold's training models)

| arm | rule | note |
|---|---|---|
| **OURS^fold** | MMR-PP[CVaR], `pearson`, λ = 0.7 (D031 / D032 star, frozen; no re-choice) on the training-models-only tensor | primary |
| **PAPER8** | LLMmap's 8 queries (fixed) | primary competitor |
| **JG^fold** | JointGreedy-CVaR (γ = 0.1) on the same restricted tensor | reference |
| **FULL** | MMR-PP[CVaR] chosen on all models (D031's chain, verbatim) | transfer-cost reference: selection that has seen the held-out models |

- `k` = 8 primary; nested prefixes `k` = 1–8 kept for curves.

### Readouts (both enrol every model from S_build-config traces only)

- **① LLMmap open-set encoder, retrained.**
  - LLMmap's own open-set (siamese) mode (`train.py`, `is_open`), shipped
    hyperparameters, no tuning.
  - Trained per fold on S_build traces of the training models only.
  - Templates for all enrolled models are built from S_build-config traces
    across configs (the config-spanning "All" library, D030 lesson), using
    LLMmap's own template aggregation.
  - Distance: ~~cosine (released default)~~ **Euclidean** (the released
    default; cosine reported alongside). *Correction (design-side,
    2026-10-22, before any data; P1 Call 1, TACC_NOTES Issue 27):* the
    released code defaults to Euclidean and the siamese head is trained on
    it; cosine was only D030's primary for LLMMAP-OS. Seeds 0–2.
- **② Nearest point cloud, training-free.**
  - For a target's `k` responses and each enrolled model m, per query q:
    point-to-cloud energy 2·E‖x − Y‖ − E‖Y − Y′‖, with Y = m's S_build cloud
    for q (frozen I5 embeddings).
  - Sum over queries; predict the argmin.
  - Variants: raw, and scale-free (each term divided by m's within-cloud
    E‖Y − Y′‖). Chosen on S_val.
  - Closest to I3. No training, so no seeds.
- **Reference row (no new runs):** the closed-set attention / linear readouts
  trained on all 85 models (D031 counts for FULL and PAPER8). This is the "if
  you could retrain" ceiling.
- **Reference row (descriptive):** D030's LLMMAP-OS (released encoder,
  PAPER8 only), as stored.

## What counts as an answer

**Targets and candidates.** Every model is held out in exactly one primary
fold. Its S_test-config traces are the held-out targets, scored against all
enrolled models (85 v2 / 37 v1). Results are pooled over folds.

**Endpoints** (S_test, `k` = 8, per view, per readout):
- **O_all:** top-1 accuracy on held-out targets.
- **O_tail:** CVaR₀.₁ over pairs (a, b), with a held out, of two-candidate
  accuracy: target from a, candidates {a, b}. Pairs with both members held
  out in the same fold average the two directions. P states the exact
  statistic, consistent with D031's two-logit accuracy.
- **Secondary:** the same on training-model targets (the seen / unseen
  gap; cf. D030 0.72 vs 0.40).

**Primary contrast** (per readout × view): Δ = OURS^fold − PAPER8. Labels
are D031's table (δ = 0.02):

| label | rule |
|---|---|
| **BETTER** | lo(Δ_all) > 0 **and** lo(Δ_tail) > 0 |
| **PARITY** | both intervals ⊂ [−0.02, +0.02] |
| **WORSE** *(headline)* | hi(Δ_all) < 0 **or** hi(Δ_tail) < 0 |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise |

- **Readout ① is primary.** It is LLMmap's own open-set mechanism, so the
  comparison holds the readout fixed exactly as D031 did. ② is reported with
  equal prominence. Its labels are a separate question, not a sign
  requirement on ①.
- **Claim rule (pre-registered):** "our selection helps open-set
  identification" = BETTER under ① in v2, with no WORSE under ① or ② in
  either view. Anything else is reported as is.
- **Bootstrap:** D023 hierarchical config × seed (seeds for ① only), 2,000
  replicates; fold assignment fixed (not resampled); D029 / D023 draw sets.

**Secondary (descriptive):**
- (a) **Transfer cost:** FULL − OURS^fold on held-out targets, per readout.
  PARITY means selection transfers to unseen models.
- (b) ① vs ② on the same chain, and each against the closed-set ceiling.
- (c) JG^fold row; curves `k` = 1–8.
- (d) Near-relative breakdown: held-out targets whose N′ / D001 sibling is
  enrolled vs not; worst-decile composition.
- (e) Family-level folds: the same endpoints, descriptive.
- (f) The mandatory all-pair hard-tail plot for held-out targets (I1).
- (g) Union size of all fold chains (`k` ≤ 8) and its overlap with D031 /
  D032 chains, for D034's generation list.

**Honesty note for R.** S_test configs were read in D029–D032 for closed-set
readouts. Open-set readouts and leave-models-out chains are new, but the
configs are not unseen. These are development numbers. Confirmation is
D034's open-set section, on fresh configs, under this D's frozen protocol.

## Constraints

- **I2:** selection on S_build; choices on S_val; S_test read once in S3.
  Enrolment uses S_build configs only.
- **I3:** selection is cloud vs cloud on the training-model tensor. ②
  compares a target deployment to each model's config cloud. The target side
  is one deployment by construction: that is the identification task, as in
  every closed-set readout.
- **Held-out exclusion is an invariant of this D:** held-out models enter
  neither selection nor ① training. Asserted in S0 and S2.
- **I4** untouched. **I5 / I6:** frozen embeddings and tensors.
  **I7:** no corpus change. **No generation.**
- **No tuning** beyond the pre-declared ② variant. If ①'s shipped recipe
  fails on our data (e.g. does not converge), raise a Call. Don't patch
  silently, and don't build a strawman (D030 / D031 lessons).
- **Out of scope:**
  - (b) rejecting unknowns, i.e. thresholds and AUROC. ① and ② output
    distances, so a later D can add it on the stored files;
  - MET / ZeroPrint open-set;
  - new models beyond the 85.
- **Cost (design-side guess):** ① ≈ 4 arms × 5 folds × 2 views × 3 seeds =
  120 encoder trainings; ② and selection are CPU. TACC measures one ①
  training in P. **Call if > 40 node-h.**
