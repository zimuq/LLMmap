## D — The question

> **Are some model pairs substantially harder to separate than others, and is that
> hardness *structured* — concentrated in fine-tune / same-family relationships —
> rather than spread uniformly across pairs?**

### Why this gates everything

CDQD rests on two premises (`METHOD.md` §2):

1. **A heavy hard tail exists.** If all pairs are comparably separable, then
   `CVaR_γ` is numerically equal to the mean, our objective is identical to
   LLMmap's, and every downstream comparison is a null experiment **that produces
   no error and no warning** (`METHOD.md` §6.4).
2. **The hard tail is structured.** Targeted generation (`METHOD.md` §5.4 step D)
   conditions a generator on a *confusion cluster*. If hard pairs are unstructured
   noise, there is no coherent cluster to condition on, and step D has no target.

Premise 1 alone justifies a new objective. Premise 1 **and** 2 justify the full
loop. If neither holds, we should know that now — before spending ~10⁵ generations
on a corpus.

### Prior expectation (state it now, so we can be wrong on record)

The paper's own worst case is Meta-Llama-3-70B-Instruct at 84%, and the confusion
matrix attributes it to Smaug-Llama-3-70B-Instruct — *a fine-tune of that model*.
We expect this to be an instance of a general pattern, not an isolated case.

**We are pre-registering this expectation deliberately.** If the data contradicts
it, that is a finding, not a failure.

---

## What counts as an answer

| Outcome | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **CONFIRMS** | The pairwise separability distribution has a distinct left tail, **and** the bottom decile is enriched in structurally-related pairs (base↔fine-tune, adjacent versions, same family) relative to their base rate among all pairs. |
| **FALSIFIES** | The bottom decile is statistically indistinguishable from a random draw over all pairs — i.e. hardness carries no structural signal. |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | Too few structurally-related pairs exist among the shipped models to detect enrichment, or the enrichment estimate's confidence interval spans "no effect". → report; do not force a verdict. |

**Report the actual distribution regardless of verdict.** The shape of that
distribution is itself an input to choosing `γ` (C1) and `θ` (C5).

---

## Required measurements

**M1 — Pairwise separability matrix** over all models shipped with the released
pretrained open-set model (`./data/pretrained_models/default`, which ships
behavioral templates for 52 LLMs). Whatever quantity that model naturally exposes
for comparing two models is acceptable here; state precisely what was used.

**M2 — Distribution** of pairwise separability: histogram + the 5th/10th/25th/50th
percentiles.

**M3 — Structural annotation of the bottom decile.** For each hard pair, label:
same base model (one is a fine-tune of the other) · adjacent versions in one
family · same organisation · same architecture family · same tokenizer · unrelated.
Report an enrichment statistic vs the base rate over all pairs, with a confidence
interval.

**M4 — The motivating case.** Does Meta-Llama-3-70B-Instruct ↔
Smaug-Llama-3-70B-Instruct land in the hard tail? Report its percentile rank.

**M5 — The ≤14B subset.** Restrict to models ≤14B and repeat M2–M3.
**This is the measurement that actually feeds D002.** Compute limits may force us
to exclude 70B-class models from the universe; if hard pairs exist *only* among
large models, invariant I1 becomes hard to satisfy and the universe decision
changes shape. Report the best ≤14B candidate confusion clusters explicitly.

## Constraints

**Applicable invariants:** none of I1–I7 bind here — this D *precedes* corpus
construction. But M5 exists specifically to determine whether I1 is satisfiable.

**Interpretation limits — carry these into R:**

- The released repo is **LLMmap0.2**, a PyTorch rewrite the README explicitly
  states is *not* a one-to-one conversion of the paper's implementation. Numbers
  may differ from published ones. Record what we observe as *our* baseline; do not
  reconcile against the paper.
- Separability measured through this model reflects hardness **under the paper's
  existing 8 queries**, not hardness in principle. That is exactly what we want —
  it tells us where the current strategy fails — but it must not be reported as
  "these models are intrinsically indistinguishable." That stronger claim belongs
  to the identifiability frontier (T2.4), which requires the full pool.
- Ground truth for "is X a fine-tune of Y" comes from model cards and naming.
  Flag any pair where the relationship is inferred rather than documented.

**Out of scope:** do not build a corpus, do not train anything, do not implement
the separability tensor or GreedyCover. This D answers a premise question only.
